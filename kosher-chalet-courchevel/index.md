Five en-suite bedrooms for 10 to 14 guests, heated pools, a hammam and a private shuttle to the lifts. Our team koshers the kitchen, a private chef cooks every meal, and Shabbat comes with a minyan and a Sefer Torah. One request, one written quote.

## The chalet, *in short*

- **Bedrooms.** Five bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, on three floors. One bedroom for each couple, 10 to 14 guests.
- **Location.** The heart of Courchevel, in the 3 Valleys. The slopes are five to six minutes on foot.
- **Shuttle.** A private shuttle runs from 9 am to 6 pm and drops you at the foot of the lifts.
- **Wellness.** A heated pool indoors and outdoors, a hammam, a relaxation area and a gym.
- **Art.** An artist's chalet: every room is dressed with a unique work of art chosen by the owner. One bedroom all white, like a snowfield; another dark, with tapestry on the walls and ski scenes. An old family farm reworked into a few chalets.
- **Also.** A large living room with a fireplace, a dining room, a home cinema, a ski room, private parking and a south-facing terrace.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A private chef cooks every meal.

Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat *in the chalet*

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the chalet. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your family, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

## The Courchevel chalet: *questions*

How much does it cost?

From €17,500 per week for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.

How many people can stay?

Five en-suite bedrooms, for 10 to 14 guests.

Who cooks the meals?

A private chef cooks kosher meals at the chalet. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.

How do we get to the slopes?

On foot in five to six minutes, or with the private shuttle from 9 am to 6 pm.
