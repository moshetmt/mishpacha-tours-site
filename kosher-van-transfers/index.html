Private van transfers
from point A to point B, never on Shabbat

One message, one driver.

Request a vanOr message us on WhatsApp

Tell us where you start, where you are going, the date, how many of you are travelling and how much luggage you have. We find a licensed driver in our network and set up a private van for your family or group, with room for bags, strollers and skis. Airport, station, hotel, ski resort or city: anywhere in France, and abroad too.

What you get

  • One message on WhatsApp. You write once and we find the driver and the vehicle.
  • A van for a family or a group. Room for the whole party, with suitcases, strollers and skis.
  • A driver from the Habad network. We ask our contact in the local Habad network, where you land or where you stay. The driver is discreet and knows the Shabbat times.
  • Never during Shabbat or on holidays. We do not book a ride that runs on Shabbat or on a Jewish holiday. We plan every transfer around the start and the end of Shabbat.
  • A quote, then a confirmed ride. There is no price list. Every transfer is quoted on request, in writing, before you commit.

The ride is carried out and invoiced by a licensed driver from our network; we find the driver and set it up for you.

How it works

  1. 1

    Send one message

    Tap the button: the message is ready. Write where you start, where you are going, the date, how many of you and how much luggage. Add your flight or train number if you have it.

  2. 2

    We find your driver

    We ask our contact in the local Habad network for a driver with a van that fits your group.

  3. 3

    You get a quote

    You receive the price in writing before anything is confirmed. The ride is carried out and invoiced by a licensed driver from our network; we find the driver and set it up for you.

  4. 4

    Your driver meets you

    Your driver is at the agreed place at the agreed time. Every ride is planned around Shabbat and the holidays.

From A to B

  • Airports and train stations. Pick-up when you land or arrive, drop-off for your departure.
  • Hotels and chalets. From your door to the next one.
  • Ski resorts. From the airport or the station straight to the resort, with the skis. See our kosher ski holidays.
  • Cities and long distances. Lyon to Paris, one city to another, or elsewhere. Beyond France, send us the route and we ask our contacts.

Shabbat and the holidays

We never run a transfer during Shabbat or on Jewish holidays. If your flight lands on a Friday afternoon or just after Shabbat, tell us: we plan the pick-up around the times, and we say plainly when a ride is not possible.

Van transfers: questions

Where can you take us?

From any point to any other point in France: airport, train station, hotel, chalet, ski resort or city. We also arrange rides abroad. Send us the route and we ask our contacts.

How much does a van transfer cost?

There is no price list. The price depends on the distance, the date, the size of the group and the luggage. You receive a written quote before you confirm.

Who drives, and who invoices?

A licensed driver from our network drives and invoices the ride. Mishpacha Tours does not sell the transport in its own name: we find the driver and set it up for you.

Can we travel on Friday evening or on Shabbat?

No. We never run a ride during Shabbat or on Jewish holidays. For a Friday arrival, we plan the pick-up around candle lighting.

Is there room for our luggage, strollers and skis?

The van is chosen for your group. Give us the number of people and what you carry (suitcases, strollers, skis) in your first message, and we find a vehicle that fits.
