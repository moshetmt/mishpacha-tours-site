# Shabbat in Lyon *how to keep it, step by step*

## At the *door*

French authorities keep security forces visible around Jewish sites, so expect a check at the entrance of a synagogue. Bring a photo ID and arrive a few minutes early. More on the [synagogues of Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/synagogues-lyon/) page.

## Shabbat *meals*

Plan your Shabbat meals before you arrive.

- **Caterers.** Seven caterers are certified by the Beth Din de Lyon. We help you choose and write. See [kosher catering in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-shabbat-meals-catering-lyon/).
- **Chabad.** A Chabad house can host you for a meal. Call before Friday noon and say how many you are. See [Chabad in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/chabad-lyon/).
- **Apartment Shabbat.** Two groceries certified by the Beth Din, Hypercacher and Lilly Market, sell what you need. Buy on Thursday: shops close on Friday afternoon.

## Where to *sleep*

Walk to prayer, walk to your meal, walk back. The Presqu'île, around Bellecour, puts you about ten minutes from the Grande Synagogue. The 6th arrondissement is the neighbourhood of most kosher restaurants on the Lyon side, about thirty-five minutes from Tilsitt on foot. Villeurbanne is the centre of community life, far from the old town. Our page [where to stay in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/where-to-stay-lyon-jewish-travellers/) gives the estimates, the map and the questions to put to a hotel about keys, floors and doors.

## What *closes* on Friday

Every kosher restaurant, bakery, butcher and grocery on the Beth Din list closes for Shabbat on Friday afternoon. Assume early, and shop on Thursday or Friday morning. Call before you plan a Saturday night dinner.

The Jewish cemetery follows the same rhythm: open until 16:00 on Friday, closed on Saturday (see [the cemetery page](https://mishpachatours.com/jewish-cemetery-lyon/)).

## Eruv *and carrying*

If you rely on an eruv at home, ask the synagogue or your host before you carry anything outdoors on Shabbat.

## Mikveh

Lyon has several mikvahs. Mikvah Haya Mouchka, 60 rue Crillon (6th arrondissement), is listed as open nightly. Addresses, phones and how to ask are on our [mikveh page](https://mishpachatours.com/mikveh-lyon/). The Consistoire also looks after the community's mikvahs: Maison du Consistoire.

## Shabbat in Lyon: *questions*

When does Shabbat start in Lyon?

The box at the top of this page shows candle lighting and Havdalah for this week. For your dates, write to us and we send the exact times.

Can I find a minyan on Shabbat?

Yes. The Grande Synagogue and Neveh Chalom hold services, and so do the Chabad houses and 25 neighbourhood minyanim. Tell us your hotel and we point you to the nearest one.

Is there a kosher hotel in Lyon?

If you find one, tell us and we update this page. Meanwhile, choose a hotel within walking distance of a synagogue and order your meals in advance.

Is there an eruv in Lyon?

Ask the synagogue or your host before you carry.

Will anyone feed me if I arrive late on Friday?

Do not count on it. Write to a Chabad house or a caterer before Thursday.

Do you guide on Shabbat?

Never. We guide from Sunday to Friday morning, and we do not run tours on Jewish holidays.
