Chalet Rochebrune, Les Deux Alpes
6 bedrooms, skis on at the door

A 165 m² chalet in Les Deux Alpes, fifty metres from the slopes: you put your skis on at the door.

Plan my stay on WhatsApp

Ski by day. Come back to a set table, a minyan, and Shabbat ready.

A private chef, or meals deliveredThe kitchen is kashered, every meal of the week is taken care of.
Shabbat and the holidays, readyChallot, wine, candles, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah on request.
One written quote, the same dayYour resort, your dates, your group. One contact from start to finish.

Six bedrooms and 13 beds, a living room with a fireplace, a south-facing terrace. Our team koshers the kitchen, a chef cooks or kosher meals are delivered, and Shabbat comes with a minyan on request. Five minutes on foot from our other chalet, for a group of two families. One request, one written quote.

The chalet, in short

  • Bedrooms. Six bedrooms for 13 guests: a bedroom with a large double bed that splits into twins, a twin bedroom, two double bedrooms, a bedroom with three single beds, and bunk beds for two kids.
  • Bathrooms. A bathroom with a tub, a shower room, two separate toilets.
  • Living. A living room with a fireplace, a dining table for everyone, an equipped kitchen, a smart TV and Wi-Fi.
  • Outside. A south-west terrace and a garden, a barbecue, a garage and two parking spaces. A laundry with a washing machine and a boot dryer.
  • Location. Les Deux Alpes, at 1,650 m. The slopes are fifty metres away, the ski school meeting point three hundred, the shops and the shuttle stop a hundred.
  • With our other chalet. Five minutes on foot from Chalet King David: two families in each house, dinner together in one.
  • Kitchen. Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

Beds made on arrival in winter, linen and towels included. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

The chalet in pictures

Shabbat in the chalet

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the chalet, or the meals arrive before candle lighting. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen.

Getting there

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. About 2 h 30 by road. Geneva, about 3 h. Grenoble, about 1 h 30.
  • Transfers. Vans with car seats from the airport to the chalet and back, on quote.
  • In the resort. The shuttle stop is a hundred metres from the door; the shuttle from Grenoble stops there too.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

Chalet Rochebrune: questions

How much does it cost?

From €13,000 per week for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.

How many people can stay?

Six bedrooms and 13 beds, for two families or one large family.

Can two families take both chalets?

Yes. Chalet King David is five minutes on foot. Four families, two houses, one chef for the dinners together.

Who cooks the meals?

A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.

How far are the slopes?

Fifty metres: you put your skis on at the door. The ski school meeting point is three hundred metres away.
