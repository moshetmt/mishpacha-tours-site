So give us your dates early. Shabbat and weekday prayers take place with Beth Habad of the Alps. A Sefer Torah is brought only if the minyan is assured. ## Your *questions*

Does the minyan cost anything?

The minyan is not free: a contribution to costs may be requested. We have no price to give you on this page. Tell us your dates, and we answer in writing before any commitment on your side. The Megillah reading is on special request, also with a contribution to costs.

Can I reach you during Shabbat?

No. We reply the same day, never during Shabbat. Send your request before Friday.

Who certifies the kosher service?

Kosher lamehadrin, certified by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany. This certificate covers the meals and the service of Mishpacha Tours.

Is a minyan guaranteed?

No. A minyan is offered on request, depending on the families present, and the Sefer Torah is brought only if the minyan is assured. The earlier you give your dates, the easier it is to form.

Is it available in summer?

Yes. A minyan is possible in every resort in summer, and on weekdays too. A Sefer Torah is brought once the minyan is assured. Tell us your resort and your dates.

Where is there a Habad house?

Courchevel 1850 and Megève have a Beth Habad on site, but the minyan there is not guaranteed outside the school holidays, when the resorts are quiet. It depends on the families present.
