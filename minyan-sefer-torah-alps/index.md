When several families stay in the same resort, our kosher team based in the Alps brings them together for a minyan and brings a Sefer Torah. It works in winter and in summer. The more families we know about, the easier the minyan is to form.

So give us your dates early. Shabbat and weekday prayers take place with Beth Habad of the Alps. Nothing is guaranteed in advance: a minyan is offered on request, depending on the families present.

## What we *arrange*

* **Gathering the families.** The team brings together the families present in your resort on your dates.
* **A Sefer Torah.** The team brings a Sefer Torah to your resort, in winter and in summer.
* **Shabbat and weekday prayers.** They take place with Beth Habad of the Alps.
* **Courchevel 1850 and Megève.** Both resorts have a Habad house in winter, which coordinates the minyan and Shabbat.
* **Shabbat meals, if you wish.** Friday dinner, Saturday lunch and seouda shlishit are available. See the Shabbat and holiday meals page.

## Your *questions*

Does the minyan cost anything?

We have no price to give you on this page. Tell us your dates, and we answer in writing before any commitment on your side.

Can I reach you during Shabbat?

No. We reply the same day, never during Shabbat. Send your request before Friday.

Who certifies the kosher service?

Kosher lamehadrin, certified by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany. This certificate covers the meals and the service of Mishpacha Tours.

Is a minyan guaranteed?

No. A minyan is offered on request, depending on the families present. The earlier you give your dates, the easier it is to form.

Is it available in summer?

Yes. The team brings a Sefer Torah in winter as in summer. Tell us your resort and your dates.

Where is there a Habad house?

Courchevel 1850 and Megève have a Habad house in winter, which coordinates the minyan and Shabbat.
