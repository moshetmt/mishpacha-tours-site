## The chalet in pictures

## The chalet, in short

- **Location.** In Courchevel 1850, at the foot of the slopes, with a view of the mountains.
- **Pool and hammam.** Below the chalet, a heated indoor pool reserved for your group, with massage seats, a hammam and a rain shower.
- **Outdoors.** On the terrace, a jacuzzi and a sauna, a cedar nordic bath and a fire pit, facing the peaks.
- **Six bedrooms,** all double, each with its own bathroom: a master suite with a mountain view, four double rooms and a VIP room with a freestanding bath.
- **Living and dining.** A living room and a dining room with a wood stove, a long table under chandeliers, and a grand piano.
- **Wellness and games.** A gym, a private massage room, a cinema with surround sound, and a games room with a billiard table and a table football.
- **Lift and ski room.** A private lift serves the five levels, and the chalet has a ski room.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes up to 12 guests, in six double bedrooms. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat in the chalet

The chef cooks your weekday, Shabbat and holiday meals in the chalet, or your meals are delivered; for Shabbat, they arrive before candle lighting. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The kitchen is certified kosher by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: [Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-pesach-french-alps/).

## Chalet Impérial: questions

- **How much does it cost?** Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.
- **How many people can stay?** Up to 12 guests, in six double bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. One large family, or several families together.
- **Are the slopes close?** The chalet stands at the foot of the slopes of Courchevel 1850.
- **What does the wellness area include?** A heated indoor pool with massage seats, a hammam and a rain shower, a gym and a private massage room. On the terrace, a jacuzzi, a sauna and a nordic bath.
- **Who cooks the meals?** A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.
- **Is there a minyan?** We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
