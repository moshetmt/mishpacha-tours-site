Alpe d'Huez, strictly kosher
Isère, 1,860 m, with a long kosher summer history

Alpe d'Huez sits in Isère at about 1,860 m, the main resort of the Grand Domaine.

Its lifts run from 5 December 2026 to 25 April 2027. We organise your kosher stay: private chef or delivered meals, Shabbat, minyan and Sefer Torah, in one written quote.

What we organise in Alpe d'Huez

  • A chalet or apartment with a private chef. Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. Every meal of the week is taken care of.
  • Meals delivered in the resort. Hot meals delivered to your apartment or your hotel for the week, for a family or a group, with notice.
  • Shabbat. Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat.
  • The holidays. Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach on the mountain. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
  • A minyan and a Sefer Torah. The size of a minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
  • A tailor-made stay, A to Z. Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our team cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; the supervision details come in writing with your quote.

Kosher in Alpe d'Huez

  • A kosher summer, then a drop. The director of the tourist office said that kosher tourism represented nearly 20 % of August visitors in 2019 and 1 % today.
  • Summer 2026. The organiser Niphla Events welcomed 250 guests in August 2026 in an establishment it rented in Alpe d'Huez.

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. 2 h 20 by road without traffic. The last stretch climbs 21 hairpin bends from Le Bourg-d'Oisans.

Kosher in Alpe d'Huez: questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything.

Can you deliver kosher meals to an apartment in Alpe d'Huez?

Yes, for a family or a group, for the week, with notice. Send your dates and the number of people; you receive a written quote.

Is there a minyan in Alpe d'Huez?

A minyan depends on how many families are there that week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Can we spend Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach in Alpe d'Huez?

Yes. Tell us which holiday falls in your week. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal are part of what we organise, and Pesach is on request.

Who supervises the kosher food?

Our team in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.
