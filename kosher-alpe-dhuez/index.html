Alpe d'Huez, strictly kosher
Isère, about 1,860 m, with a long kosher summer history

Alpe d'Huez sits in Isère at about 1,860 m, the main resort of the Grand Domaine, and its lifts run from 5 December 2026 to 25 April 2027 according to the resort. Kosher tourism made up nearly 20 % of its August clientele in 2019, per the tourist office director. Mishpacha Tours organises the kosher side of your stay here: a private chef in your chalet, hot meals delivered to your apartment, Shabbat, the holidays, and a minyan and a Sefer Torah for your dates. One request, one written quote. Stays, transfers and meals are organised and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps; Mishpacha Tours sells its guided tours in Lyon.

What we organise in Alpe d'Huez

  • A chalet or apartment with a private chef. Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. Every meal of the week is taken care of.
  • Meals delivered in the resort. Hot meals delivered to your apartment or your hotel for the week, for a family or a group, with notice.
  • Shabbat. Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat.
  • The holidays. Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach on the mountain. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
  • A minyan and a Sefer Torah. The size of a minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
  • A tailor-made stay, A to Z. Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our team cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote.

Kosher in Alpe d'Huez: what is published

  • A kosher summer, then a drop. The director of the tourist office said that kosher tourism represented nearly 20 % of August visitors in 2019 and about 1 % today. Source: AFP, relayed by Sud Radio, 23 August 2026.
  • Summer 2026. The same article reports that the organiser Niphla Events welcomed 250 guests in August 2026 in an establishment it rented in Alpe d'Huez.
  • Winter programmes. We found no kosher hotel or apartment programme for Alpe d'Huez in the sources read (search of 8 October 2026). Not published.
  • Kosher shop or restaurant in the resort. Not published.

Supervision is rarely published. Ask before you book, and ask for it in writing.

Minyan and Shabbat in Alpe d'Huez

No Chabad house is published in Alpe d'Huez. Our kosher team based in the Alps covers the Grenoble side of the massif, and a minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week: give us your dates.

Shabbat times: check Hebcal for Alpe d'Huez and your week, and set your meals before candle lighting. For the summer, see summer in the Alps for Jewish families.

Getting to Alpe d'Huez

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. About 2 h 20 by road without traffic, according to j2ski.com; transfer operators quote 2 h 15 to 2 h 35 (alps2alps.com). The last stretch climbs 21 hairpin bends from Le Bourg-d'Oisans.
  • From Grenoble. About 1 h by road.
  • Shuttles. Winter shuttles from the airport are listed on Lyon, the gateway to the Alps. Transfers can be part of a tailor-made stay, on quote.
  • Kosher groceries before you climb. Lyon has certified shops: what to buy and where.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.
