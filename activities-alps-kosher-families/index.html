Skis, dog sled, a car, transfers: on request, on a single written quote.

Summer outingsLakes, walks, Annecy, Aix-les-Bains and Grenoble.
One written quoteEverything you ask for appears on a single quote, sent before any commitment.

Your stay does not stop at the chalet. Tell us what your family would enjoy and we add it to the same quote: skis to rent, a ride behind a dog sled, a car for the week, transfers to and from the airport or station. Each activity is on request, so nothing is booked before you have approved it.

In summer, the Alps open onto lakes and walks. Annecy, Aix-les-Bains and Grenoble are close, and a villa in Saint-Tropez is possible. We plan no activity on Shabbat or on holidays. Whatever is not listed here, ask us and we check.

What we can arrange

  • Ski rental. Skis for the family, on request, collected in your resort. Tell us sizes and dates, they appear on your quote.
  • Dog sled. A ride behind a team of sled dogs, on request. Tell us your resort and we tell you what we can arrange.
  • Rental car. A car for the length of your stay, on request, added to the same quote as your chalet.
  • Transfers. A private van with a licensed driver from the network, to or from the airport, station, hotel or resort. Never during Shabbat or holidays.
  • Extras for events. A family occasion during your stay? Tell us what you have in mind and we tell you what is possible.
  • Summer outings. Lakes, walks, Annecy, Aix-les-Bains, Grenoble, and a villa in Saint-Tropez if you wish.

Your questions

Which activities can you arrange?

On request, on one quote: ski rental, dog sled, rental car, transfers and extras for events. In summer: lakes, walks, Annecy, Aix-les-Bains, Grenoble and a villa in Saint-Tropez. For anything else, ask us and we check.

How much do they cost?

There is no published price grid for activities. Each one is priced on your written quote, with the price for your dates, before any commitment. Transfers are carried out and billed by the driver.

Are activities possible on Shabbat?

No. We plan no activity on Shabbat or on holidays, and transfers are never run on those days. Tell us which holiday falls during your week and we plan around it.

Can I book lift passes or instructors?

We have not confirmed these. Tell us what you need, we check with the team and answer in writing. Ski rental is available on request.

Can the whole stay stay kosher?

Yes. Meals and service are Kosher lamehadrin, certified by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany. Meals are Chalav Yisrael and service is shomer Shabbat, at the chalet or delivered.
