## Your *questions*

Which activities can you arrange?

On request, on one quote: ski rental, dog sled, snowmobile, mountain guide, rental car, transfers and event organisation. In summer: lakes, walks, Annecy, Aix-les-Bains, Grenoble and a villa in Saint-Tropez. For anything else, ask us and we check.

How much do they cost?

Everything is on request and no price is published. Each activity is priced on your written quote, with the price for your dates, before any commitment. Transfers are carried out and billed by the driver.

Are activities possible on Shabbat?

No. We plan no activity on Shabbat or on holidays, and transfers are never run on those days. Tell us which holiday falls during your week and we plan around it.

Can I book lift passes or instructors?

A mountain guide is possible on request. For lift passes and ski instructors, nothing is confirmed: tell us what you need, we check with the team and answer in writing. Ski rental is available on request.

Can the whole stay stay kosher?

Yes. Meals and service are Kosher lamehadrin, certified by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany. Meals are Chalav Yisrael and service is shomer Shabbat, at the chalet or delivered.
