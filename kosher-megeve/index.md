## What we organise *in Megève*

- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** The team makes the kitchen kosher, the chef cooks on site, every meal of the week is covered. You ski, you come back to a set table.
- **Meals delivered in the resort.** Hot meals brought to your chalet, apartment or hotel for the week. Families, friends or a group: send the number of people and the dates.
- **Shabbat before candle lighting.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine are delivered on Friday and set before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **The holidays.** Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal, Pesach on request. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** Outside it, the minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week: give us your dates and we group them.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; the supervision details come in writing with your quote. Detail of each service: [kosher services in the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-services-french-alps/).

## Getting *to Megève*

- **From Geneva airport:** 1 h 15 by road. Transfer companies give 1 h 05 to 1 h 30 depending on traffic and weather.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport:** 2 h 10 without traffic. Megève Tourisme gives 2 h 15. On a Saturday in February, or in snow, add an hour or more.
- **Opening dates, winter 2026-2027:** ski area open from 19 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, with partial openings from 5 and 12 December.

If you land in Lyon, shop kosher before you climb: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/). Or stop for three hours in the city: [the Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/).
