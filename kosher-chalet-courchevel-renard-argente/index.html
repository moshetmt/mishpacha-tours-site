Courchevel

Kosher chalet Renard Argenté
Courchevel, two suites, Nordic bath

Price on request

2 suites · Nordic bath

Request the catalogue WhatsApp

Ski by day. Come back to a set table, a minyan, and Shabbat ready.

Two suites, up to 6 guests. An outdoor Nordic bath heated to 40 °C, a wood stove, a private cinema. The snow front is 400 metres away, with a shuttle in winter. A private chef, Shabbat and minyan, arranged for you.

  • A private chef, or meals deliveredThe kitchen is kashered, every meal of the week is taken care of.
  • Shabbat and the holidays, readyChallot, wine, candles, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah on request.
  • One written quote, the same dayYour resort, your dates, your group. One contact from start to finish.

The chalet, in short

  • Location. In a private hamlet of Courchevel Village, at 1,550 metres, among the larch trees. The village centre and the snow front are 400 metres away; in winter, a shuttle takes you to the slopes.
  • The living room. Under the beams, with a wood stove, wide windows onto the mountains and a home cinema. The kitchen and the dining table are on the same floor.
  • Two suites, each with its own bathroom, one with a walk-in shower. The living room welcomes two more guests. A guest WC.
  • Nordic bath. A wooden bath outside, heated to 40 °C, on the landscaped terrace. A balcony on the other side.
  • Service. Housekeeping included, safes in the suites, a shuttle to the slopes in winter.
  • Kitchen. Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes 2 to 6 guests. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

Shabbat in the chalet

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the chalet, or the meals arrive before candle lighting. The dining table seats the whole group. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen.

How it works

  1. Tell us Your dates, the number of guests and what you want. By WhatsApp or with the form at the bottom of this page. Hebrew-speaking guests are welcome.
  2. We check the chalet Our team in the Alps confirms the chalet for your dates, then the chef, the transfers and the minyan.
  3. You get one programme A day-by-day programme and one written quote for the whole stay, before you commit to anything.

Getting there

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. About 2 h 15 to 2 h 30 by road. Geneva, about 2 h 30.
  • Transfers. Vans with car seats from Geneva or Lyon to the chalet and back, on quote.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

Chalet Renard Argenté: questions

  • How much does it cost? Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.
  • How many guests? Two suites, and the living room welcomes two more guests: up to 6. Made for a couple, two couples or a young family.
  • How far are the slopes? The village centre and the snow front are 400 metres away. In winter, a shuttle takes you to the slopes and back.
  • What is the Nordic bath? A wooden bath outside on the terrace, heated to 40 °C. You sit in hot water with the snow all around.
  • Who cooks the meals? A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.
  • Is there a minyan? We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
