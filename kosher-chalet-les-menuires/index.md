## The chalet, *in short*

- **The setting.** A quiet hamlet just below Les Ménuires, in the heart of the 3 Valleys and their 600 km of slopes. The chairlift, 230 metres from the door, takes you up in seven minutes; in the evening, you ski back to the chalet.
- **The living floor.** A 50 m² living room open onto the kitchen and a south-facing balcony, with one table for the whole group.
- **Seven suites,** each with its own shower room, over three floors: privacy for every family, up to 15 guests.
- **The spa.** An outdoor jacuzzi under the snow, a sauna with its own shower, and a games and cinema lounge for the children.
- **Ski room.** A large ski room with boot dryers, at the door.
- **Service.** Fully renovated. Linen, beds made on arrival and housekeeping at departure, included.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes up to 15 guests, from Sunday to Sunday. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat *in the chalet*

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the chalet, or the meals arrive before candle lighting. The dining table seats the whole group. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

## Chalet Aigle Royal: *questions*

How much does it cost?

Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.

How many guests?

Up to 15 guests in seven suites, each with its own shower room: two or three families together, or one family across generations.

How do we reach the slopes?

The chairlift, 230 metres from the chalet, takes you up to Les Ménuires in seven minutes. At the end of the day, a blue run brings you back to the door.

Is there a spa?

Yes: an outdoor jacuzzi, a sauna with its own shower, and a games and cinema lounge.

Who cooks the meals?

A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
