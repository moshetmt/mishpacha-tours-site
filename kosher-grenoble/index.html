Grenoble, strictly kosher
your base for the Isère ski resorts

Grenoble sits at 214 m, between the Chartreuse and Vercors massifs and the Belledonne range, and it is the city from which our kosher team based in the Alps reaches the resorts of the Isère: Chamrousse, Alpe d'Huez, Les 2 Alpes and beyond. Mishpacha Tours organises the kosher side of your week from here: a private chef in your chalet, hot meals delivered to your apartment, Shabbat before candle lighting, the holidays, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah for your dates. One request, one written quote. Stays, transfers and meals are organised and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps; Mishpacha Tours sells its guided tours in Lyon.

What we organise from Grenoble

  • A chalet or apartment with a private chef. Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site, in Grenoble or in the resort where you stay. Every meal of the week is taken care of.
  • Meals delivered for the week. Hot meals brought to your apartment or hotel, for a family or a group, with notice. The team works from Grenoble and travels up to the resort.
  • Shabbat. Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday and set before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
  • The holidays. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal, Pesach on request. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
  • A minyan and a Sefer Torah. A Chabad house is published in Grenoble (see below). For a resort, the minyan depends on how many families are there that week: give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
  • Large groups. Meals for a group for the whole week, in a hall or in the apartments, are part of what the team does. Send the number of people and the dates.
  • A tailor-made stay, A to Z. Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote. Detail of each service: kosher services in the French Alps.

Grenoble as your base for the Isère resorts

Grenoble is the largest city in the Alps, and a single request covers the resorts around it. Drive times from the city, according to Sport 2000 (read 8 October 2026):

  • Chamrousse. About 53 minutes by car.
  • Alpe d'Huez. About 1 h 13 by car.
  • Les 2 Alpes. Jet2 gives 114 km from Grenoble-Alpes-Isère airport. No drive time from the city is published in the sources we read.
  • Serre Chevalier. We organise kosher services there too, from Grenoble. Ask us for your dates.

Other resorts have their own page: Alpe d'Huez, Les 2 Alpes, Val Thorens, Courchevel and Megève.

Kosher in Grenoble: what is published

  • Beth Habad de Grenoble. Listed on Chabad.org at 10 rue Lazare Carnot, 38000 Grenoble. A prayer schedule is published on the centre's site, with weekday services and a Saturday morning service; the page is old, so confirm the times for your dates.
  • Grande Synagogue of Grenoble. 11 rue André Maginot, 38000 Grenoble, according to jguideeurope.org and PagesJaunes.
  • Bar Yohai synagogue. 4 rue des Bains, 38000 Grenoble, according to jguideeurope.org, where it is tied to the Consistoire Régional de Lyon.
  • Kosher shop, butcher or restaurant. None published in the sources we read. Bring your food from Lyon, or let us deliver meals to you.

Supervision is rarely published. Ask before you book, and ask for it in writing. Public addresses only; no phone number is given on this page.

Minyan and Shabbat in Grenoble

Grenoble has a Chabad house and two synagogues (addresses above). For your dates, tell us where you are staying: in the city, we point you to the nearest service; in a resort, we build the minyan with the Chabad network of the Alps and bring a Sefer Torah.

Shabbat times change every week. Check them for Grenoble and your dates on Hebcal, and set your meals before candle lighting.

Getting to Grenoble

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport, by road. About 90 km and about an hour, according to FlixBus.
  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport, by train. Direct TGV trains cover 77 km in about 1 h 04, five a day according to Omio (checked 8 October 2026). FlixBus runs a bus in about 1 h 05 to the Grenoble bus station.
  • From Lyon Part-Dieu by TER. About 1 h 23, according to SNCF Connect.
  • From Paris. Direct TGV in about 3 h (SNCF Connect, shortest trip 2 h 59).
  • Grenoble-Alpes-Isère airport. At Saint-Étienne-de-Saint-Geoirs, about 40 minutes from the city by road. Its busy period runs from December to April according to Vinci Airports. There is no rail link.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road. Kosher groceries before you climb: Lyon, the gateway to the Alps.

Kosher in Grenoble: questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything. We publish no price.

Can you deliver kosher meals to an apartment near Grenoble or in a resort?

Yes, for a family or a group, for the week, with notice. Send your dates, the resort and the number of people; you receive a written quote.

Is there a minyan in Grenoble?

A Chabad house is listed in the city, and two synagogues. In a resort, a minyan depends on how many families are there that week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Can you cover several resorts from Grenoble?

Yes. Our team works from Grenoble for the resorts of the Isère. Tell us which one and which dates.

Can we spend Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach in the Alps?

Yes. Tell us which holiday falls in your week. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal are part of what we organise, and Pesach is on request.

Who supervises the kosher food?

Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. Ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote.
