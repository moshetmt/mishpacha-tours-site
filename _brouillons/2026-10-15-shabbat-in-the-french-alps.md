## 4. Station par station

### Courchevel
Rabbi Daniel Belaïch holds Chabad services in winter at the Hôtel Le Mont Charvin, 389 rue des Tovets, 73120 Courchevel. He writes that he helps with kosher needs, wine and challot for Shabbat. A minyan in your own chalet or hotel depends on how many families are in the resort that week: give us your dates and we group them, with a Sefer Torah. From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry, 2 h 10 without traffic.
[Kosher Courchevel](/kosher-courchevel/)

### Val Thorens
At 2,300 m in Les 3 Vallées, Val Thorens has no standing minyan in our sources. We build one for your dates with the Chabad network of the Alps, and bring a Sefer Torah. Shabbat meals, challot and wine arrive on Friday before candle lighting. From Lyon airport, 2 h 30 by road without traffic.
[Kosher Val Thorens](/kosher-val-thorens/)

### Megève
The Chabad of the Alps runs a winter presence at 136 route Edmond de Rothschild, 74120 Megève; confirm the venue before you come. Outside it, the minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week. Shabbat meals are set before candle lighting, nothing is delivered on Shabbat. 2 h 10 from Lyon, 1 h 15 from Geneva.
[Kosher Megève](/kosher-megeve/)

### Alpe d'Huez
Alpe d'Huez (1,860 m, Isère) has no standing service. Our kosher team covers the Grenoble side of the massif and builds the minyan from the families present. Grenoble is 1 h by road, Lyon airport 2 h 20, with 21 hairpin bends from Le Bourg-d'Oisans on the last stretch.
[Kosher Alpe d'Huez](/kosher-alpe-dhuez/)

### Les Deux Alpes
A glacier resort at 1,650 m, 2 h 10 from Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. Kashrut falls under the supervision of the Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes. The minyan depends on the families in the resort that week; our chalet there (6 bedrooms, 16 beds) is organised with chef, Shabbat meals and a minyan on request.
[Kosher Les Deux Alpes](/kosher-les-deux-alpes/) · [Our chalet](/kosher-chalet-les-deux-alpes/)

### Val d'Isère
A chalet resort at 1,850 m, 220 km and 2 h 30 to 3 h from Lyon airport. The last 32 km from Bourg-Saint-Maurice are mountain road, slow in snow: plan Friday arrivals with a wide margin before candle lighting. We bring a Sefer Torah for a minyan built on your dates.
[Kosher Val d'Isère](/kosher-val-disere/)

### Les Arcs
Four villages (Arc 1600, 1800, 1950, 2000) above Bourg-Saint-Maurice. No standing minyan; we build one from the families who write to us for your dates, with a Sefer Torah. Tell us which village you sleep in. The funicular from Bourg-Saint-Maurice reaches Arc 1600 in 7 minutes.
[Kosher Les Arcs](/kosher-les-arcs/)

### La Plagne
Eleven villages, 195 km and about 2 h 15 from Lyon airport. The minyan is built with the Chabad network of the Alps for the dates you give, with a Sefer Torah. Choose your village with the kitchen and the lifts in mind, then tell us.
[Kosher La Plagne](/kosher-la-plagne/)

### Chamonix
No kosher hotel, restaurant or shop in the valley in our sources. The nearest Chabad presence we know of is in Megève, 35 km away. Minyan and Sefer Torah on your dates, winter or summer. 1 h 15 to 1 h 30 from Geneva, 2 h 15 to 3 h from Lyon.
[Kosher Chamonix](/kosher-chamonix/)

### Annecy
The small synagogue at 18 rue de Narvik is open on Shabbat and holidays only. The nearest Chabad house is in Annemasse, 40 minutes away. A visiting minyan has to be built ahead, for the lake in summer or La Clusaz and Le Grand-Bornand in winter. 1 h 15 from Lyon airport, 45 minutes from Geneva.
[Kosher Annecy](/kosher-annecy/)

### Grenoble
The one base with a Chabad house and two synagogues: Beth Habad at 10 rue Lazare Carnot, Grande Synagogue at 11 rue André Maginot, Bar Yohai at 4 rue des Bains. The Chabad network of the Alps is headed from here. About 1 h from Lyon airport.
[Kosher Grenoble](/kosher-grenoble/)

## 5. How we organise Shabbat in your resort

1. **A chef or delivered meals.** A private kosher chef cooks in your chalet, or hot meals are delivered to your apartment or hotel for the week, with notice.
2. **Meals set before candle lighting.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine are delivered on Friday. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
3. **Warming equipment on request.** Hot plate and urn for Shabbat can be requested in the planning form. À confirmer : modalités par station.
4. **A minyan and a Sefer Torah on request.** Give your dates early: the more families we know about, the easier the minyan. We build it with the Chabad network of the Alps.
5. **Arrive well before Shabbat.** Mountain roads slow down on Saturdays and in snow. Plan to be in the resort with a wide margin before candle lighting, and tell us your arrival day so the first meal is planned around it.

## 6. FAQ (prête pour FAQPage)

**Q1. Where can we find a minyan on Shabbat in the French Alps?**
Courchevel 1850 and Megève have Chabad services in winter, run by Rabbi Daniel Belaïch. In the other resorts there is no standing service in our sources, so we build a minyan from the families present on your dates and bring a Sefer Torah. Grenoble has a Chabad house and two synagogues.

**Q2. Can we get Shabbat meals in the resort?**
Yes, for a family or a group, with notice. Meals, challot and wine are delivered on Friday before candle lighting, or a private chef cooks in your chalet. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat itself.

**Q3. Who supervises the food?**
Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

**Q4. Is there a kosher shop or restaurant in the resorts?**
Do not count on one in most resorts. Bring groceries from the certified shops of Lyon, or let us deliver meals to you.

**Q5. When should we arrive before Shabbat?**
Well before candle lighting, which falls as early as 16:30 in December. Roads are slower on a Saturday changeover and in snow, and Val d'Isère is a long mountain climb from Bourg-Saint-Maurice.

**Q6. Do you sell a Shabbat package?**
No package and no price list. Each stay is tailor-made and you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. Send your resort, dates and number of people on WhatsApp or through the planning form.
