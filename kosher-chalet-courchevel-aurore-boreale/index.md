## The chalet, *in short*

- **Location.** In a private hamlet of Courchevel Village, at 1,550 metres, among the larch trees. The village centre and the snow front are 400 metres away; in winter, a shuttle takes you to the slopes.
- **The living room.** Open and bright, with a hanging fireplace in the middle, a long wooden table for the meals and wide windows onto the trees. Two terraces face south.
- **Three suites,** each with its own bathroom: a bath with jets, a hammam shower, a bath and shower. The TV lounge turns into a fourth bedroom. A guest WC.
- **Pool, hammam, hot tub.** The outdoor pool is heated all year, with a counter-current to swim against. A hammam and a hot tub for the end of the ski day.
- **Service.** Housekeeping included, a ski room at the entrance, a shuttle to the slopes in winter.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes 6 to 8 guests. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat *in the chalet*

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the chalet, or the meals arrive before candle lighting. The dining table seats the whole group. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

## Chalet Aurore Boréale: *questions*

How much does it cost?

Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.

How many people can stay?

6 to 8 guests: three suites, and the TV lounge turns into a fourth bedroom. One family, or two small families together.

How far are the slopes?

The village centre and the snow front are 400 metres away. In winter, a shuttle takes you to the slopes and back.

Is the pool heated in winter?

Yes. The outdoor pool is heated all year, with a counter-current. A hammam and a hot tub complete it.

Who cooks the meals?

A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
