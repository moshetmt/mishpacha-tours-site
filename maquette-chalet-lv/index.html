Chalet Belle Étoile

From €9,500 per week

Plan your stay WhatsApp Talk to our concierge

Strictly kosher, Chalav Yisrael. A day-by-day programme and a written quote the same day, never on Shabbat.

A private wooden chalet above Val Thorens, facing south, with a ski path at the door and the village a few minutes away on foot. The chef cooks every meal in the kashered kitchen, or kosher meals are delivered. Shabbat is ready when you come back from the slopes: challot, wine, candles, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah on request, in your living room.

The chalet
  • 165 m² on three floors, facing south
  • Three large en-suite bedrooms, up to 10 guests (8 adults)
  • Living room with stove and bay window, dining table for ten
  • Ski room, balconies over the valley
Included with your stay
  • Private chef, or kosher meals delivered
  • Kitchen kashered before you arrive
  • Shabbat and the holidays ready, minyan with a Sefer Torah on request
  • One contact in the Alps, from the quote to departure
Location and access
Val Thorens, in the 3 Valleys. Geneva airport about 2 h 30 by private transfer, Lyon about 2 h 30. Transfers arranged on request, never on Shabbat.
Questions
  • Who cooks? A private chef, or meals delivered by the Beth Habad of the Alps.
  • Is there a minyan? Yes, on request, with the men of your group and the bochurim we bring.
  • How do we book? Send your dates; you receive a day-by-day programme and a written quote the same day.
