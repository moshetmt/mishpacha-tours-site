Guide to Les Ménuires

Shabbat in Les Ménuires, in practice

The minyan, in your chalet. No permanent minyan and no eruv are published in the resort. When your group, or the families we bring together that week, make up the number, the minyan is held in the chalet, and we bring a Sefer Torah on request.

Val Thorens is not a Shabbat walk. It lies several kilometres further up the valley by road, so families who keep Shabbat stay in Les Ménuires from candle lighting to Havdalah.

No kosher shop in the resort. No kosher grocery or restaurant is published in Les Ménuires: shop in Lyon before you drive up (our Lyon guide), or let the chef and the delivered meals take care of the week.

Getting to Les Ménuires

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. By road to Moûtiers, then 27 km up the Belleville valley to the resort. Allow extra time on a Saturday in February or in snow.
  • Shuttles. Altibus runs regular buses from the airports and from Moûtiers station; details on Lyon, the gateway to the Alps. Transfers can be part of a tailor-made stay, on quote.
  • Kosher groceries before you climb. Lyon has certified shops: what to buy and where.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

Kosher in Les Ménuires: questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything.

Can you deliver kosher meals to an apartment in Les Ménuires?

Yes, for a family or a group, for the week, with notice. Send your dates and the number of people; you receive a written quote.

Is there a minyan in Les Ménuires?

A minyan depends on how many families are there that week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Can we spend Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach in Les Ménuires?

Yes. Tell us which holiday falls in your week. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal are part of what we organise, and Pesach is on request.

Who supervises the kosher food?

Our team in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

Can we walk to a minyan in Val Thorens on Shabbat?

No. Val Thorens is several kilometres further up the valley by road, and no eruv is published. On Shabbat, the minyan takes place in your chalet when the group or the families gathered that week make up the number, with a Sefer Torah on request.

Where is the kosher chalet in Les Ménuires?

Our kosher chalet in Les Ménuires is the Chalet Aigle Royal: seven suites, a spa with jacuzzi and sauna, up to 15 guests, seven minutes from the slopes by chairlift.
