## What we organise *anywhere*

- **A private kosher chef who travels with you.** Our team based in the Alps sends a chef to your chalet or apartment, makes the kitchen kosher and cooks every meal of the week on site. The kosher shopping travels with the chef or is bought from the nearest Jewish community.
- **Shabbat and the holidays.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine set before candle lighting; Hanukkah, Purim, Pesach or Sukkot in the mountains. Nothing is prepared on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **A minyan.** Where a synagogue or a Chabad house exists in the resort, we give you the times. Where none does, a minyan is built from the families who write to us for the same week, with a Sefer Torah on request.
- **Transfers.** From the nearest airport to the door of the chalet, on days that respect Shabbat and the holidays.
- **The chalet or apartment.** We find it with you, or we work with the one you have already booked.
- **One contact.** The same WhatsApp number as for the Alps, from the first message to the last transfer.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. Detail of each service: [kosher services in the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-services-french-alps/).

## Kosher anywhere: *questions*

Which destinations do you cover?

Austria, North America and any ski resort not on our list, in addition to the resorts of France, Switzerland and Italy already on this site. Tell us the resort and we tell you what is feasible.

How does the kosher shopping work far from the Alps?

Part of it travels with the chef, part is bought from the nearest kosher shop or Jewish community. We plan it before you leave, and the supervision details come with your quote.

Can you organise a minyan in a resort with no synagogue?

A minyan depends on how many families are there that week. Give us your dates early and we group the families who write to us, with a Sefer Torah on request.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the destination, the dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit.

Do you work in summer as well?

Yes. Walking, lakes, festivals: the services are the same in summer, chef, Shabbat, holidays, minyan.

Who supervises the food?

Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come with your quote.
