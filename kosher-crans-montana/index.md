# Crans-Montana *strictly kosher, above the Rhône*

Crans-Montana sits on a south-facing plateau at about 1,500 m in the Valais, with a glacier reaching 2,927 m.

## What we organise *in Crans-Montana*

- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. In winter you come back from the Plaine Morte runs to a set table; in summer, from the golf course or the lakes.
- **Meals delivered on the plateau.** Hot meals brought to your chalet, apartment or hotel for the week, for a family or a group, with notice. Tell us if you sleep in Crans, in Montana or in one of the smaller villages on the plateau, because the road between them adds minutes in snow.
- **Shabbat.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday and set before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **The holidays.** Hanukkah, Purim, Pesach or Sukkot in the Valais. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** The resort has a synagogue with its own seasons, and the size of a minyan depends on how many families are on the plateau that week. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers from Geneva, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core, including large groups and Hebrew-speaking guests. For a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

## Kosher in Crans-Montana

Supervision is rarely written down, so check before you book.

- **Kosher hotel.** One hotel on the plateau carries a kosher listing, but its own page limits kosher meals to private religious groups and mentions a closure for renovation. Do not plan a family stay around it.
- **Kosher restaurant or shop.** None found in the resort. Plan to bring your food or have it delivered.
- **Synagogue.** A synagogue stands on Rue Centrale and opens by season: from 1 December to 31 March in winter and from 1 July to 31 August in summer, with weekday prayers and Shabbat services. Those dates are from a 2024 document, so we confirm them for your week.
- **Nearest large communities.** Lausanne is about 1 h 30 away and Geneva about 2 h, both with community restaurants and caterers. Plan any shopping there before the week starts.
- **Season.** The lifts are announced from 19 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, with a partial opening from 28 November if snow allows. The FIS Alpine World Ski Championships take place in Crans-Montana from 1 to 14 February 2027: book early for those dates.

## Winter and summer *on the plateau*

- **Winter.** About 140 km of runs, mostly facing south, climb from the villages at 1,500 m to the Plaine Morte glacier. Sunset comes early on a Friday in December, so Shabbat meals are set well before the lifts close.
- **Summer.** The resort is known for its golf course, which hosts a professional tournament each year, for its lakes and for its walking. The Plaine Morte lift runs from April to November, weather permitting. Packed lunches for the trail can be prepared with notice.
- **Minyan and Shabbat.** The synagogue on Rue Centrale is open in winter and in summer only, so a week in spring or autumn needs a minyan built from the families who write to us, with a Sefer Torah. Shabbat times appear live on the Alps pages of this site.

## Getting *to Crans-Montana*

- **From Geneva airport:** about 2 h by road along the A9 in the Rhône valley, leaving at Sierre-Ouest, then about 25 minutes up to the plateau. Direct trains run from the airport to Sierre in about 2 h 10.
- **From Sierre station:** the funicular takes 13 minutes to the resort, and a red line on the pavement leads from the platform to its station, about 5 minutes on foot.
- **From Sion:** about 25 minutes by car, or a PostBus of about 50 minutes.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry:** about 3 h 30 to 3 h 45 by road through Geneva. Lyon suits long-haul flights and a first kosher shop before you climb: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
- **Crossing from France:** carry a passport or identity card for every traveller, children included, and buy the Swiss motorway vignette online before the border, as the A9 is a vignette motorway.
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.
