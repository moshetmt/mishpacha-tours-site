## What we organise *in Annecy*

- **A villa, chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. Every meal of the stay is covered, by the lake in summer or after a day on the slopes in winter.
- **Meals delivered to Annecy and the Aravis.** Hot meals brought to your villa, apartment or hotel for the week, in town, around the lake or in La Clusaz and Le Grand-Bornand. Send the number of people and the dates.
- **Shabbat before candle lighting.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine are delivered on Friday and set before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **The holidays.** Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal, Pesach on request. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** Annecy has a small synagogue (see below), but a minyan in your week depends on how many families are in the area. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Villa or chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Large groups and families from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; the supervision details come in writing with your quote. Detail of each service: [kosher services in the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-services-french-alps/).

## Summer on the lake, *winter in the Aravis*

- **Summer.** Lake Annecy is 14.6 km long and covers 27.6 km², at 447 m (Wikipedia). Families stay in town, in villas around the lake or in the hills, from June to August. Chef or delivered meals, Shabbat in a rented house, a minyan for a week: tell us your address and dates.
- **Winter: La Clusaz.** The village is at 1,040 m and the ski area reaches 2,600 m. Skiinfo shows an opening on 12 December 2026 and a closing on 25 April 2027, to be confirmed by the resort. 30 to 33 km and 37 minutes by road from Annecy.
- **Winter: Le Grand-Bornand.** The village is at 1,000 m, Chinaillon at 1,300 m, and the ski area tops out at Mont Lachat, 2,100 m. La Clusaz and Le Grand-Bornand are linked by a shuttle and a common lift pass.
- **Semnoz.** The Semnoz massif above Annecy rises from 450 m to 1,699 m, a day trip for a family staying in town.

A stay in Annecy often combines the lake and the slopes. One request covers both.

## Getting *to Annecy*

- **From Geneva airport:** 45 to 50 km and 45 minutes by the A41. Add time on summer weekends and in snow.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport:** 125 km and 1 h 15 by road, via the A43 and A41. Transfer companies give up to 1 h 22.
- **Annecy airport (Meythet):** 35 km and 45 minutes from La Clusaz.
- **To La Clusaz from Geneva:** 56 to 70 km, 50 minutes to 1 h 15.
