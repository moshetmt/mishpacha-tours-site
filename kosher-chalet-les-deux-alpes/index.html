Les Deux Alpes · Certified kosher

Kosher chalet King David
Les Deux Alpes, 300 m², private chef

From €13,500 per week

6 + 1 bedrooms · 2 min from the slopes

Request the catalogue WhatsApp

Ski by day. Come back to a set table, a minyan, and Shabbat ready.

Six bedrooms and 16 beds, for three small families or one large one. A private chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or meals are delivered; Shabbat and the holidays come with a minyan and a Sefer Torah, depending on the families present. One request, one written quote.

  • A private chef, or meals deliveredThe kitchen is kashered, every meal of the week is taken care of.
  • Shabbat and the holidays, readyChallot, wine, candles, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah on request.
  • One written quote, the same dayYour resort, your dates, your group. One contact from start to finish.

Where you sleep, what you eat, where you ski

  • Chalet or apartment We find the place for your group, from a family chalet to several apartments in one residence.
  • Chef or delivered meals A private kosher chef in your kitchen, or hot meals delivered to your door, Shabbat and holidays included.
  • Ski and the glacier Passes, rental and lessons on request, in a resort that skis up to the glacier.

The chalet, in short

  • Surface. 300 m².
  • Bedrooms and beds. 6 bedrooms, 16 beds: three small families or one large family.
  • Location. Les Deux Alpes, Isère, a resort at 1,650 m. 2 minutes from the slopes, with direct access to the ski area.
  • Living room. A living room for the whole group.
  • Kitchen. Made kosher by our team for your stay. Strictly kosher meals, cooked on site by a private chef or delivered to the chalet.

Dates: ask for your dates. The chalet is offered in winter (Hanukkah, the February holidays, Pesach) and in summer.

Everything around the chalet

Our kosher team based in the Alps organises the stay from A to Z. You choose what you need, we take care of the rest.

  • Flights. Plane tickets for your group.
  • Transfers and taxi. From the airport to the chalet and back, and rides in the resort.
  • Ski rental. Skis and equipment for the whole family.
  • A private chef and groceries. Meals cooked at the chalet, and the shopping done before you arrive.
  • Masseur and pedicure at home. They come to the chalet.
  • Car rental.
  • Dog sledding and other activities. On request.
  • Event extras. For a celebration or a group gathering in the chalet, on request.

One request, one written quote.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen.

Shabbat and the holidays in the chalet

Shabbat meals are cooked at the chalet by the chef or delivered to it. The holidays are organised too: Hanukkah, the February holidays and Pesach. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.

A minyan and a Sefer Torah depend on the families present that week. Give us your dates early and we tell you what is possible.

Strictly kosher. The kitchen is certified kosher by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany.

How it works

  1. Tell us Your dates, the number of people and the services you want. By WhatsApp or with the form at the bottom of this page. Hebrew-speaking guests are welcome.
  2. We put it together We check with our team in the Alps what is possible for your dates: chalet, chef, transfers, Shabbat, activities. One contact from start to finish.
  3. You get one quote A single written quote for the whole stay, before you commit to anything.

Getting there

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. 2 h 10 by road in dry conditions. Add time on a winter Saturday or in snow.
  • Shuttles and taxi. Airport shuttles are covered on Lyon, the gateway to the Alps. Transfers and taxi can be part of your stay, on quote.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

Chalet King David: questions

  • How much does it cost? From €13,500 per week for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.
  • How many people can stay? 16 beds in 6 bedrooms, so three small families or one large family.
  • Who cooks the meals? A private chef cooks kosher meals at the chalet, or meals are delivered to it. Our team makes the kitchen kosher for your stay. The kitchen is certified kosher by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany.
  • Is Shabbat organised? Yes. Shabbat meals and the holidays (Hanukkah, February, Pesach) are organised. A minyan and a Sefer Torah depend on the families present that week: give us your dates early.
  • Can you arrange activities? Yes, on request: ski rental, dog sledding, a masseur or pedicure at the chalet, car rental, transfers and event extras. Everything appears on one quote.
