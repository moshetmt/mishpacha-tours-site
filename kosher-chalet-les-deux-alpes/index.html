A kosher chalet in Les Deux Alpes
300 m², 15 beds, 2 minutes from the slopes

A chalet of 300 m² in Les Deux Alpes (Isère, resort at 1,650 m), with 7 bedrooms and 15 beds: room for three small families or one large family. It stands 2 minutes from the slopes, with direct access to the ski area. Kosher meals are cooked in the chalet by a private chef or delivered to it. Shabbat and the holidays are organised, with a minyan and a Sefer Torah depending on the families present. Our team organises the whole stay, from the flights to the extras. One request, one written quote. Stays, transfers and meals are organised and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps; Mishpacha Tours sells its guided tours in Lyon.

The chalet, in short

  • Surface. 300 m².
  • Bedrooms and beds. 7 bedrooms, 15 beds: three small families or one large family.
  • Location. Les Deux Alpes, Isère, a resort at 1,650 m. 2 minutes from the slopes, with direct access to the ski area.
  • Living room. A living room for the whole group.
  • Kitchen. Made kosher by our team for your stay. Strictly kosher meals, cooked on site by a private chef or delivered to the chalet.

Dates: ask for your dates. The chalet is offered in winter (Hanukkah, the February holidays, Pesach) and in summer.

Everything around the chalet

Our kosher team based in the Alps organises the stay from A to Z. You choose what you need, we take care of the rest.

  • Flights. Plane tickets for your group.
  • Transfers and taxi. From the airport to the chalet and back, and rides in the resort.
  • Ski rental. Skis and equipment for the whole family.
  • A private chef and groceries. Meals cooked at the chalet, and the shopping done before you arrive.
  • Masseur and pedicure at home. They come to the chalet.
  • Car rental.
  • Dog sledding and other activities. On request.
  • Event extras. For a celebration or a group gathering in the chalet, on request.

One request, one written quote.

Shabbat and the holidays in the chalet

Shabbat meals are cooked at the chalet by the chef or delivered to it. The holidays are organised too: Hanukkah, the February holidays and Pesach. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.

A minyan and a Sefer Torah depend on the families present that week. Give us your dates early and we tell you what is possible. Shabbat times: check Hebcal for Les Deux Alpes and your week.

Strictly kosher. Ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote.

Getting there

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. About 2 h 10 by road in dry conditions, according to j2ski.com; other sites give 2 h 20 to 2 h 30. Add time on a winter Saturday or in snow.
  • Shuttles and taxi. Airport shuttles are covered on Lyon, the gateway to the Alps. Transfers and taxi can be part of your stay, on quote.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

The kosher chalet: questions

How much does it cost?

There is no price list. The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: chef, transfers, ski rental, activities. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything.

How many people can stay?

15 beds in 7 bedrooms, so three small families or one large family.

Who cooks the meals?

A private chef cooks kosher meals at the chalet, or meals are delivered to it. Our team makes the kitchen kosher for your stay. Ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote.

Is Shabbat organised?

Yes. Shabbat meals and the holidays (Hanukkah, February, Pesach) are organised. A minyan and a Sefer Torah depend on the families present that week: give us your dates early.

Can you arrange activities?

Yes, on request: ski rental, dog sledding, a masseur or pedicure at the chalet, car rental, transfers and event extras. Everything appears on one quote.
