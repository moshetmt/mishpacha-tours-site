Each chalet can come with its own team, on quote: a private chef, and the meals of the week and of Shabbat. The kitchen is kashered before you arrive. You ski, you walk, you rest. When you come back, the table is set.

Each chalet has its own weekly price and its own amenities, from heated pools and a hammam in Courchevel to a sauna and a jacuzzi near Megève. A chalet without meals is possible on request, and so is the kashering of the kitchen of a chalet you rent elsewhere. A PDF catalogue of the chalet is sent on request.

## What your chalet *includes*

* **A kashered kitchen.** The kitchen of the chalet is kashered before you arrive, so from the first evening everything you use is kosher.
* **A private chef, on quote.** The chef cooks on site and takes care of all the meals of the week, so nobody has to cook on holiday. Priced separately from the chalet.
* **Weekday and Shabbat meals, on quote.** Meals are served at the chalet or delivered, during the week and on Shabbat. Tell us your tastes and your family's needs.
* **The chalet's own amenities.** Depending on the chalet: heated pools and hammam, outdoor jacuzzi, sauna, Nordic bath, games room and cinema, or a spa facing Mont Blanc.
* **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** When several families stay in the same resort, the team brings them together for a minyan and brings a Sefer Torah. Give us your dates early.
* **One contact, one written quote.** One person follows your stay from start to finish. You receive a written quote before any commitment, and we reply the same day, except on Shabbat.

## Your *questions*

How much does a private kosher chalet cost?

Prices are per chalet and per week, for the chalet alone. They start from €9,500 for Belle Étoile (Val Thorens) and from €23,500 for Loup Blanc (near Megève). The private chef and the meals come on top, on quote. The price for your dates is given to you in writing with the quote.

Who prepares the meals?

A private chef cooks on site, in the kashered kitchen of the chalet, and takes care of all the meals of the week. The chef is priced on quote, on top of the chalet. Meals are served at the chalet or delivered.

Can we spend Shabbat at the chalet?

Yes. Shabbat meals are possible, at the chalet or delivered, on quote on top of the chalet. Give us your dates and we tell you the nearest minyan. We do not reply during Shabbat.

Who certifies the kosher?

Kosher lamehadrin, certified by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany. The meals are Chalav Yisrael and the service is shomer Shabbat.

Can I rent the chalet without meals?

Yes: the price shown is for the chalet alone, and the chef and the meals are added on quote if you want them. We can also kasher the kitchen of a chalet you rent elsewhere. Tell us what you need and we send you a written quote.
