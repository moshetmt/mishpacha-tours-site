Zermatt sits at about 1,600 m in the Valais, at the foot of the Matterhorn.

## What we organise *in Zermatt*

- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. You come back from the Matterhorn slopes to a set table.
- **Meals delivered in the village.** Hot meals brought to your chalet, apartment or hotel for the week, for a family or a group, with notice. Zermatt has no private cars, so tell us exactly where you sleep and how your luggage and food reach the door.

## Kosher in Zermatt

Kosher supervision in a Swiss resort is rarely announced, so plan before you book.

- **Kosher hotel, restaurant or shop in Zermatt.** Plan to bring your food or have it delivered. A search that returns nothing is not proof that nothing exists, and we keep checking.
- **Chabad house or synagogue in the village.** The Chabad centres we know of in Switzerland are in the large cities, Geneva and Zurich among them, far from the Matterhorn.
- **Season.** The lifts of the Matterhorn Ski Paradise ran from 1 November to early May in the 2025-2026 winter. The lift company sets the dates for winter 2026-2027: check them before you book a lift pass.

## A village *without cars*

- **Täsch, then the train.** Drivers stop at the Matterhorn Terminal in Täsch, about 8 km below Zermatt, and take the shuttle train. It runs about every 20 minutes and takes about 12 minutes to the village station.
- **Electric taxis.** Only electric vehicles and horse-drawn carriages move guests and goods inside the village. We plan the delivery of your meals around them, with notice.
- **Luggage and food.** Everything that arrives by road is unloaded at Täsch or at the station. Tell us your chalet or hotel and we plan the last stretch.

## Winter, summer *and Cervinia*

- **Winter.** The Matterhorn Ski Paradise links Zermatt with Cervinia and Valtournenche on the Italian side, with roughly 320 to 360 km of pistes depending on the count. Meals are timed around your ski day and the early sunset.
- **Summer skiing.** On the Theodul Glacier, the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise at 3,883 m keeps up to 21 km of pistes for summer skiing, mostly useful in the early morning. In July and August the village is also a base for walkers.
- **Across to Italy.** The crossing to Cervinia takes you out of Switzerland: carry your passport on the slopes, and see [kosher in Cervinia](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-cervinia/) if you sleep on the Italian side.

## Getting *to Zermatt*

- **Frontier and road.** Switzerland is outside the European Union: carry your passport at the border. A car on a Swiss motorway needs the vignette, a sticker or e-vignette sold at the border or online.
- **From Geneva airport:** about 240 km to Täsch, 3 to 3 h 30 by road, then 12 minutes by train. The route runs along Lake Geneva to the Rhône valley, through Visp.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport:** about 350 km to Täsch, around 4 h to 4 h 30 by road via Geneva. Lyon suits long-haul flights and a first kosher shop before you drive on: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
- **From Zurich:** about 3 h 30 by train with changes; Zurich is the other large Swiss airport, with a Chabad presence in the city.
- **From Milan-Malpensa:** about 190 km, around 3 h by road over the Simplon pass and through Brig, with a border crossing. Sion, the small airport of the Valais, is about an hour from Täsch.
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.
