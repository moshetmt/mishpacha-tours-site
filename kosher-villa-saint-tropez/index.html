A kosher villa on the water in Saint-Tropez
private beach, boat, chef and Shabbat

Villa Beach sits with its feet in the water, in the gulf of Saint-Tropez.

Plan my tripOr message us on WhatsApp

It is the only villa for rent in Saint-Tropez with a private sand beach and its own boat garage with a launching ramp. Five bedrooms and five bathrooms for 12 guests, a heated pool and a jacuzzi in the garden, a hammam and a sauna over the sea. Our team koshers the kitchen, a private chef cooks every meal, and Shabbat is kept in the villa with a minyan if you wish.

The villa, in short

  • Three houses. The main villa, a guest house across the garden and the caretaker's lodge: 520 m² of house on 1 600 m² of land, 12 guests in five bedrooms with five bathrooms.
  • Sea view. The four bedrooms of the main villa look at the sea. A vast living room and a very large panoramic terrace overlook the water and the village of Saint-Tropez.
  • Wellness. A heated 25 m² pool and a jacuzzi in the garden, a hammam and a sauna over the sea.
  • The beach. A private sand beach at the foot of the villa and a boat garage with its own launching ramp, the only ones for rent in Saint-Tropez.
  • Quiet. The villa is calm: the only sound is the sea. A concierge is on site all year.
  • Kitchen. Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A private chef cooks every meal.

Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

Shabbat by the sea

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the villa. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your family, the minyan is at home, on the terrace over the water. The synagogue of Saint-Tropez is a walk of about 30 to 40 minutes along the sea, for those who prefer to go.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote. Summer weeks and Pesach are both possible: tell us your dates.

The sea, from the garden

  • Your own boat. A rigid inflatable boat is at your disposal, with access to the pontoon. The port of Saint-Tropez is three minutes away by boat.
  • On the water. Pedal boat, canoe, paddle board, Seabob and snorkelling masks are in the boat garage, ready to use.
  • Under the water. The villa faces a marine reserve: Mediterranean fish a few metres from the beach. This corner of the gulf is known for its calm sea.
  • Around. A sailing club 500 metres away. Bicycles take you along the protected cycle path to the port of Saint-Tropez in eight minutes.

Getting there

  • From Nice airport. About 1 h 30 to 2 h by road, depending on the season. Toulon-Hyères airport, about 1 h.
  • Transfers. Vans with car seats from the airport to the villa and back, on quote.
  • By boat. From the pontoon of the villa, the port of Saint-Tropez is three minutes away.

Villa Beach: questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything.

How many people can stay?

Five bedrooms and five bathrooms, for 12 guests, between the main villa and the guest house.

Who cooks the meals?

A private chef cooks kosher meals at the villa. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the villa for Shabbat, on request. The synagogue of Saint-Tropez is also within walking distance along the sea.

Is the beach really private?

Yes. A private sand beach at the foot of the villa, with a boat garage and a launching ramp. The sea in front of the villa is a marine reserve.

Can we come for Pesach?

Yes. Pesach and the summer weeks are both possible. Tell us your dates early.
