- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site, in Plagne Centre, Belle Plagne, Montchavin or the village you chose. Every meal of the week is covered.

## The 11 villages *and where to stay*

The official La Plagne site counts 11 villages. Altitudes below come from the resort and from access guides.

- **Plagne Centre** 1,970 m: the hub of the ski area, where the tourist office sits.
- **Plagne Bellecôte** 1,930 m, **Plagne 1800**, 1,800 m, and **Plagne Aime 2000**, 2,100 m, the highest village of the resort.
- **Belle Plagne**, **Plagne Soleil** and **Plagne Villages**, 2,050 m: three villages at the same level, Plagne Villages being described by the resort as family-oriented.
- **Montchavin, Les Coches, Montalbert and Champagny-en-Vanoise** Lower down the mountain, between 1,250 and 1,450 m.

Choose your village with the kitchen and the lifts in mind, then tell us: our team plans the chef or the deliveries around it.

## Getting *to La Plagne*

- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport:** 195 km and 2 h 14 by road without traffic. The resort site gives 196 km from Lyon.
- **From Geneva airport:** 197 km and 2 h 20. Add time on a Saturday changeover or in snow.
- **By train:** Aime-La Plagne station is on the valley floor, 18 to 30 km from the villages. The resort site lists the S71 bus from the station to the high villages such as Plagne 1800 and Aime 2000, and the S70 to Montalbert; taxis also run.
- **Opening dates, winter 2026-2027:** the high villages (Plagne Altitude) open from 12 December 2026 to 24 April 2027, and Champagny, Montchavin-Les Coches and Montalbert from 12 December 2026 to 17 April 2027.

If you land in Lyon, shop kosher before you climb: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/). Or stop for three hours in the city: [the Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/).

## Paradiski: *La Plagne and Les Arcs*

The Vanoise Express cable car links Montchavin-Les Coches to Peisey-Vallandry in Les Arcs, in under four minutes, 380 m above the valley floor. Together the two resorts form Paradiski, 425 km of linked pistes. Your kosher base can stay in La Plagne while the family skis across to Les Arcs for the day.
