La Plagne, strictly kosher
11 villages, one request for the kosher side

La Plagne spreads over 11 villages, from Champagny-en-Vanoise in the valley to Plagne Aime 2000 at about 2,100 m, and joins Les Arcs through the Vanoise Express to form the 425 km Paradiski area. Mishpacha Tours organises the kosher side of your week here: a private chef in your chalet, hot meals delivered to your apartment, Shabbat before candle lighting, the holidays, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah for your dates. One request, one written quote. Stays, transfers and meals are organised and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps; Mishpacha Tours sells its guided tours in Lyon.

What we organise in La Plagne

  • A chalet or apartment with a private chef. Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site, in Plagne Centre, Belle Plagne, Montchavin or the village you chose. Every meal of the week is covered.
  • Meals delivered in the resort. Hot meals brought to your apartment or hotel for the week. Tell us which village you stay in, as La Plagne is spread over several levels and the delivery depends on it.
  • Shabbat before candle lighting. Shabbat meals, challot and wine are delivered on Friday and set before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
  • The holidays. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal, Pesach on request. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
  • A minyan and a Sefer Torah. The size of a minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
  • A tailor-made stay, A to Z. Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad, Hebrew-speaking guests included, are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote. Detail of each service: kosher services in the French Alps.

The 11 villages and where to stay

The official La Plagne site counts 11 villages. Altitudes below come from the resort and from access guides.

  • Plagne Centre, about 1,970 m: the hub of the ski area, where the tourist office sits.
  • Plagne Bellecôte, about 1,930 m, Plagne 1800, 1,800 m, and Plagne Aime 2000, about 2,100 m, the highest village of the resort.
  • Belle Plagne, Plagne Soleil and Plagne Villages, about 2,050 m: three villages at the same level, Plagne Villages being described by the resort as family-oriented.
  • Montchavin, Les Coches, Montalbert and Champagny-en-Vanoise, lower down the mountain, between about 1,250 and 1,450 m.

Choose your village with the kitchen and the lifts in mind, then tell us: our team plans the chef or the deliveries around it.

Minyan and Shabbat in La Plagne

No Chabad house is published in La Plagne. If you want a minyan for a given week, tell us your dates early: we build it with the Chabad network of the Alps and bring a Sefer Torah.

Shabbat times change every week. Check them for your dates on Hebcal and set your meals before candle lighting.

Getting to La Plagne

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport: about 195 km and 2 h 14 by road without traffic, according to alps2alps.com. The resort site gives 196 km from Lyon.
  • From Geneva airport: about 197 km and 2 h 20, according to alps2alps.com. Add time on a Saturday changeover or in snow.
  • By train: Aime-La Plagne station is on the valley floor, 18 to 30 km from the villages. The resort site lists the S71 bus from the station to the high villages such as Plagne 1800 and Aime 2000, and the S70 to Montalbert; taxis also run.
  • Opening dates, winter 2026-2027: the high villages (Plagne Altitude) open from 12 December 2026 to 24 April 2027, and Champagny, Montchavin-Les Coches and Montalbert from 12 December 2026 to 17 April 2027 (en.la-plagne.com, checked 8 October 2026; snow conditions can change them).
  • Summer: the high villages are open from 4 July to 29 August in the 2026 calendar; the 2027 summer dates are not yet published.

If you land in Lyon, shop kosher before you climb: Lyon, the gateway to the Alps. Or stop for three hours in the city: the Stopover.

Paradiski: La Plagne and Les Arcs

The Vanoise Express cable car links Montchavin-Les Coches to Peisey-Vallandry in Les Arcs, in under four minutes, 380 m above the valley floor (alps2alps.com). Together the two resorts form Paradiski, 425 km of linked pistes. Your kosher base can stay in La Plagne while the family skis across to Les Arcs for the day.

Kosher in La Plagne: questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on the dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit. We publish no price.

Can you deliver kosher meals to an apartment in La Plagne?

Yes, for a family or a group, for the week, with notice. Send your village, your dates and the number of people.

Is there a kosher hotel in La Plagne?

Kosher directories list organised summer stays there for 2026, with a supervising rabbi named by each organiser. We found no winter programme. Ask for the supervision in writing before you book.

Is there a minyan in La Plagne?

No Chabad house is published in the resort. A minyan depends on how many families are there that week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Can you organise Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach in La Plagne?

Yes, on request. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal, Pesach meals. Ask early: these weeks fill up first.

Who supervises the food?

Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. Ask for the supervision details in writing with your quote.
