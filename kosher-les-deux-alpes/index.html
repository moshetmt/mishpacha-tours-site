What we organise in Les Deux Alpes

  • A chalet or apartment with a private chef. Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. Every meal of the week is taken care of.
  • Meals delivered in the resort. Hot meals delivered to your apartment or your hotel for the week, for a family or a group, with notice.
  • Shabbat. Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat.
  • The holidays. Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach on the mountain. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
  • A minyan and a Sefer Torah. The size of a minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
  • A tailor-made stay, A to Z. Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Kashrut under the supervision of the Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

Kosher in Les Deux Alpes

  • A kosher summer season. Orthodox Jewish families have come to Les Deux Alpes in summer for decades, and a Beth Loubavitch seminar of several hundred people, held over three weeks, moves between Alpine resorts.
  • Whole hotels. Kosher organisations often rent entire hotels in the Alpine resorts for the summer. No hotel or programme is named for Les Deux Alpes.

Minyan and Shabbat in Les Deux Alpes

Our own minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week: give us your dates. For a minyan, the team works with the Beth Habad of the Alps.

Getting to Les Deux Alpes

  • From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry. 2 h 10 by road in dry conditions. Add time on a winter Saturday or in snow.
  • Shuttles. Airport shuttles are covered on Lyon, the gateway to the Alps. Transfers can be part of a tailor-made stay, on quote.
  • Kosher groceries before you climb. Lyon has certified shops: what to buy and where.
  • Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare? The Stopover: a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

Kosher in Les Deux Alpes: questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything.

Can you deliver kosher meals to an apartment in Les Deux Alpes?

Yes, for a family or a group, for the week, with notice. Send your dates and the number of people; you receive a written quote.

Is there a minyan in Les Deux Alpes?

A minyan depends on how many families are there that week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Can we spend Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach in Les Deux Alpes?

Yes. Tell us which holiday falls in your week. Hanukkah candles, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal are part of what we organise, and Pesach is on request.

Who supervises the kosher food?

Our team in the Alps cooks under the supervision of the Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.
