## The chalet, *in short*

- **Ski in, ski out.** In Tignes, at the heart of the Espace Killy. The slope is less than a minute on foot from the door; at the end of the day, you ski back to the chalet when the snow allows.
- **Two chalets that connect.** Two adjoining chalets, connected inside and taken together: each family has its own chalet, and everyone meets at the table. Three floors in each.
- **Nine suites,** nine shower rooms, up to 21 guests, each suite with a balcony or the terrace. A family suite with a bunk room for the children, a master suite under the roof with an extra single bed.
- **Two jacuzzis.** Two outdoor jacuzzis facing the peaks, for the end of the ski day.
- **Two living rooms, two tables.** In each chalet, an open living room with a fireplace and corner sofas, a fitted kitchen and a table for twelve.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The two chalets welcome up to 21 guests together. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Chalet Étoile Polaire: *questions*

How much does it cost?

Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.

How many guests?

Up to 21 guests in nine suites, each with its own shower room: several families together, each in its own chalet, connected inside.

How far is the slope?

Less than a minute on foot from the door. At the end of the day, you ski back to the chalet when the snow allows.

Can we rent only one of the two chalets?

The two chalets are rented together, for one group. Each has its own kitchen, its own living room and its own table for twelve.

Who cooks the meals?

A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
