Ski by day. Come back to a set table, a minyan, and Shabbat ready.

Two adjoining chalets that connect inside, rented whole or by half: nine suites, each with its own shower room, for up to 21 guests. The slopes are one minute away on foot. An outdoor jacuzzi facing the peaks, two living rooms with a fireplace. A private chef, Shabbat and minyan, arranged for you.

- **A private chef, or meals delivered**The kitchen is kashered, every meal of the week is taken care of.
- **Shabbat and the holidays, ready**Challot and wine, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah on request.
- **One written quote, the same day**Your resort, your dates, your group. One contact from start to finish.

## The chalet, in short

- **One minute from the slopes.** In Tignes, at the heart of the Espace Killy. The slopes are less than a minute on foot from the chalet.
- **Two chalets that connect.** Two adjoining chalets, connected inside, rented whole or by half: each family has its own chalet, and everyone meets at the table. Three floors in each.
- **Nine suites,** nine shower rooms, up to 21 guests, each suite with a balcony or the terrace. A family suite with a bunk room for the children, a master suite under the roof with an extra single bed.
- **Jacuzzi.** An outdoor jacuzzi facing the peaks, for the end of the ski day.
- **Two living rooms, two tables.** In each chalet, an open living room with a fireplace and corner sofas, a fitted kitchen and a table for twelve.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The two chalets welcome up to 21 guests together. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat in the chalet

The chef prepares the Shabbat and holiday meals in the chalet, or the meals arrive before candle lighting. Two tables for twelve, one in each chalet. A private minyan and a Sefer Torah on request: tell us your dates early.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: [Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-pesach-french-alps/).

## How it works

1. **Tell us** Your dates, the number of guests and what you want. By WhatsApp or with the form at the bottom of this page. Hebrew-speaking guests are welcome.
2. **We check the chalet** Our team in the Alps confirms the chalet for your dates, then the chef, the transfers and the minyan.
3. **You get one programme** A day-by-day programme and one written quote for the whole stay, before you commit to anything.

## Getting there

- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry.** About 2 h 45 by road. Geneva, about 2 h 45 to 3 h.
- **Transfers.** Vans with car seats from Geneva or Lyon to the chalet and back, on quote.
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

## Chalet Étoile Polaire: questions

- **How much does it cost?** Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.
- **How many guests?** Up to 21 guests in nine suites, each with its own shower room: several families together, each in its own chalet, connected inside.
- **How far are the slopes?** Less than a minute on foot from the chalet.
- **Can we rent only one of the two chalets?** Yes: the whole Chalet Étoile Polaire, or one of the two chalets on its own, Grande Ourse (four suites) or Petite Ourse (five suites). Each has its own kitchen, its own living room and its own table for twelve.
- **Who cooks the meals?** A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.
- **Is there a minyan?** We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
