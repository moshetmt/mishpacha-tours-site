## Everything kosher, *one request*

- **A chalet with a private chef.** The team makes the kitchen kosher, the chef cooks on site, every meal of the week is taken care of. You ski, you come back to a set table.
- **Meals delivered to your group.** Families, friends, a yeshiva or a synagogue group: hot meals delivered to your apartments or your hotel, for the week, in the resort. Large groups welcome, with notice.
- **Shabbat in the resort.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday before candle lighting, and the nearest minyan for your dates.
- **The holidays in the mountains.** Hanukkah candles and a lighting, a Megillah reading and a Purim meal, Pesach on request. Say which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** When several families are in the same resort, the team groups them for a minyan and brings a Sefer Torah, in winter and in summer. Give your dates early.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core; for a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

Strictly kosher. Our team in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps; the supervision details come in writing with your quote. Shomer Shabbat: nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday, everything is set before candle lighting. ## Kosher in the Alps: *questions*

Do you sell the ski holiday itself?

No. You book your flights, your transfer and your accommodation yourself. We organise the kosher side in the resort through our team in the Alps, who quote and invoice it.

Can you deliver kosher meals to an apartment in Val Thorens or Alpe d'Huez?

Yes, for a family or a group, for the week, with notice. Send your resort, your dates and the number of people; you receive a written quote.

Is there a minyan in the resort?

Courchevel 1850 and Megève have a Chabad house in winter. Elsewhere, a minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week: give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Is the food strictly kosher?

Yes. Our team in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

How far ahead should we ask?

As early as you have your dates. Hanukkah, the February school holidays, Purim and Pesach fill up first. A minyan is easier to build when the team knows the families early.

Can you organise the whole stay?

Yes. Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: the more complete the request, the better the team can work. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.

We land in Lyon on the way up. Is there something to do?

Yes. A private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon with the Grande Synagogue, between the plane and the drive to the resort, 390 € per group: [the Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/). Kosher shopping before you climb is on [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
