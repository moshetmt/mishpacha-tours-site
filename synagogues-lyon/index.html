Synagogues in Lyon
Where the community prays, and when Shabbat comes in

Lyon has seven Chabad houses, two large historic synagogues and 25 neighbourhood minyanim, most of them in Villeurbanne. For prayer, a Shabbat meal or any question on the ground, start with a Chabad house: they welcome travellers every day. Visitors are welcome at prayer everywhere: arrive a few minutes early, have your ID with you, and expect a security check at the door, as in every synagogue in France today. For a minyan near your hotel, message us.

  • 7 Chabad houses first
  • 2 historic synagogues
  • 25 neighbourhood minyanim

Shabbat in Lyon

  • Candle lighting. Lyon's Shabbat times follow the sun: as early as 16:30 in December, after 21:00 in June. We send you the exact times for your dates when you book.
  • Our tours and Shabbat. We run Sunday to Friday morning, never on Shabbat or Jewish holidays. Friday tours end early enough to be home well before candle lighting.
  • Hospitality. For a Shabbat meal with the community, contact a Chabad house: see our Chabad in Lyon page.
