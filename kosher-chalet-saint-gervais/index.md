Ski by day. Come back to a set table, a minyan, and Shabbat ready.

Eight bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, and two children's dormitories, for up to 16 guests. An indoor pool heated all year, a sauna, a cinema room, a fireplace and a garage. Two kitchens: meat and dairy stay apart. Chef or delivered meals, Shabbat and minyan.

- **A private chef, or meals delivered**The kitchen is kashered, every meal of the week is taken care of.
- **Shabbat and the holidays, ready**Challot, wine, candles, and a minyan with a Sefer Torah on request.
- **One written quote, the same day**Your resort, your dates, your group. One contact from start to finish.

## The chalet, in short

- **Location.** Saint-Gervais Mont-Blanc, facing the Mont Blanc range. The chalet is a few steps from the ski lifts.
- **The living room.** Large sofas around the fireplace, a long dining table and the Mont Blanc range through the picture windows.
- **Eight bedrooms,** each with its own bathroom: two master rooms, two parental suites with a children's dormitory, four double rooms.
- **Pool and sauna.** An indoor pool heated to 28 °C all year, with a sauna beside it.
- **Cinema and games.** A private cinema room, and a games room with a table football for the children.
- **Ski room and garage.** A ski room with heated lockers, and a private garage.
- **Two kitchens.** Meat and dairy stay apart. Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes up to 16 guests. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat in the chalet

The chef cooks your weekday, Shabbat and holiday meals in the chalet, or your meals are delivered; for Shabbat, they arrive before candle lighting. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: [Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-pesach-french-alps/).

## How it works

1. **Tell us** Your dates, the number of guests and what you want. By WhatsApp or with the form at the bottom of this page. Hebrew-speaking guests are welcome.
2. **We check the chalet** Our team in the Alps confirms the chalet for your dates, then the chef, the transfers and the minyan.
3. **You get one programme** A day-by-day programme and one written quote for the whole stay, before you commit to anything.

## Getting there

- **From the airport.** Transfers from Geneva or Lyon-Saint-Exupéry can be arranged on request.
- **Transfers.** Vans with car seats from Geneva or Lyon to the chalet and back, on quote.
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

## Chalet Voie Lactée: questions

- **How much does it cost?** Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.
- **How many people can stay?** Up to 16 guests: eight bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, and two children's dormitories. One large family, or several families together.
- **Are the slopes far?** The chalet is a few steps from the ski lifts. Access to the slopes is confirmed with each request.
- **Is the pool heated in winter?** Yes. The indoor pool is heated to 28 °C all year, with a sauna beside it.
- **Who cooks the meals?** A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.
- **Is there a minyan?** We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
