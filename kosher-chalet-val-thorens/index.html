The chalet, in short

  • Location. Above the village of Val Thorens, the highest resort in Europe, in a quiet area facing south. The centre and the restaurants are a few minutes on foot. A ski path starts in front of the door: good skiers ski back to the chalet, the others walk the last 100 metres.
  • The living floor. 60 m² on the first floor: a lounge, a dining room with a table for ten, an open high-end kitchen, a rotating wood stove, wide south-facing windows and a balcony.
  • Three large bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. The master suite of 28 m² under the roof, with a dressing room, a mezzanine, a balcony and a safe. A bedroom of 18 m² with two single beds, a bath, a shower and a south balcony. A bedroom of 15 m² with a double bed, a shower and a south balcony. Three bathrooms and one separate WC.
  • Ski room. With boot warmers, at the door.
  • Comforts. Wifi, a washing machine, a tumble dryer, a baby cot and a high chair.
  • Kitchen. Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes up to 10 guests, 8 adults at most. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

Shabbat in the chalet

The chef prepares the Shabbat meals and the holidays in the chalet, or the meals arrive before candle lighting. The dining table seats ten. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The supervision details come in writing with your quote.

The Val Thorens Chalet: questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose. You receive a written quote before you commit to anything.

How many people can stay?

Up to 10 guests, 8 adults at most, in three en-suite bedrooms: one large family, or two families with children.

Is it really ski in, ski out?

A ski path starts in front of the door. Good skiers ski back to the chalet; for the others, the last 100 metres are on foot.

Can we walk to the restaurants?

Yes. The village centre and the restaurants are a few minutes on foot from the chalet.

Who cooks the meals?

A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.

Is there a minyan?

We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
