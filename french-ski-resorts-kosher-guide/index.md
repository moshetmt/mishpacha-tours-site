## How to choose *your resort*

Start with the level of the youngest skier in the family. Resorts with a high share of easy slopes (Les Deux Alpes, La Plagne) suit beginners and children; Tignes and Val d'Isère suit confident skiers. Then look at the road: on a Friday, the drive has to end well before candle lighting, and a winter Saturday or a snowfall can add an hour. Lyon-Saint-Exupéry is about two hours from most resorts; Geneva is closer to Megève, Chamonix and Annecy.

## The resorts, *one by one*

### Val Thorens

- **Altitude.** 2,300 m, the highest ski resort in Europe, in Les 3 Vallées.
- **Ski level.** Les 3 Vallées count 600 km of slopes, 56 % easy, 34 % intermediate, 10 % difficult. Every level finds its runs.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 30 without traffic. From Geneva: ask us for the transfer time.
- **The kosher side.** Private chef or delivered meals, Shabbat and the holidays; a minyan when enough families are in the resort that week.

[Kosher Val Thorens](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-val-thorens/)

### Courchevel

- **Altitude.** About 1,850 m for Courchevel 1850, with its lower villages, in Les 3 Vallées.
- **Ski level.** The same 600 km area as Val Thorens, 56 % easy slopes.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 10 without traffic. From Geneva: ask us for the transfer time.
- **The kosher side.** A Chabad house in winter, which we coordinate with for your minyan and Shabbat; chef or delivered meals in Courchevel 1850 and the villages.

[Kosher Courchevel](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-courchevel/)

### Les Deux Alpes

- **Altitude.** 1,650 m, with a glacier above the resort.
- **Ski level.** 220 km of slopes, 65 % easy, the highest share in this guide. Good for beginners and families.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 10 in dry conditions. From Geneva: ask us for the transfer time.
- **The kosher side.** Orthodox families have come here in summer for decades; our kosher chalets are in the resort, with a chef or delivered meals.

[Kosher Les Deux Alpes](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-les-deux-alpes/)

### Megève

- **Altitude.** About 1,050 m, on the Évasion Mont-Blanc area.
- **Ski level.** The Megève and Saint-Gervais slopes count 185 km, 37 % easy and 48 % intermediate. Families and relaxed skiers.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 10 without traffic. From Geneva: 1 h 15 by road.
- **The kosher side.** A Chabad house in winter; for a minyan in your week, we build it with the Chabad network of the Alps and bring a Sefer Torah.

[Kosher Megève](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-megeve/)

### Alpe d'Huez

- **Altitude.** About 1,860 m, the main resort of the Grand Domaine.
- **Ski level.** 250 km of slopes, 52 % intermediate. Best for skiers who already link their turns.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 20, the last stretch up 21 hairpin bends. From Grenoble: 1 h. From Geneva: ask us.
- **The kosher side.** A long kosher summer history; our team covers the Grenoble side of the massif.

[Kosher Alpe d'Huez](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-alpe-dhuez/)

### Chamonix

- **Altitude.** The town sits at about 1,040 m, at the foot of Mont Blanc; the ski areas climb to 2,767 m at Grands Montets.
- **Ski level.** Separate high mountain areas (Brévent and Flégère, Grands Montets, Les Houches, Le Tour). Our source does not publish a level split for the valley.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 15 to 3 h. From Geneva: 1 h 15 to 1 h 30, the closest airport.
- **The kosher side.** No kosher hotel or shop in the valley; we deliver meals or send a chef, in winter and in summer.

[Kosher Chamonix](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-chamonix/)

### La Plagne

- **Altitude.** 11 villages, up to Plagne Aime 2000 at about 2,100 m.
- **Ski level.** 225 km of slopes, about 62 % easy, linked to Les Arcs in Paradiski (425 km). Good for families.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 14 without traffic. From Geneva: 2 h 20.
- **The kosher side.** Tell us your village, as deliveries are planned village by village.

[Kosher La Plagne](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-la-plagne/)

### Les Arcs

- **Altitude.** Four villages, Arc 1600, 1800, 1950 and 2000.
- **Ski level.** 200 km of slopes, about half of them easy, linked to La Plagne.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 25 to 3 h to Bourg-Saint-Maurice, then the climb. From Geneva: 2 h 30 to 3 h.
- **The kosher side.** No kosher shop or restaurant in the resort in winter; chef or delivered meals, and a minyan when enough families are there.

[Kosher Les Arcs](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-les-arcs/)

### Val d'Isère

- **Altitude.** 1,850 m, sharing 300 km of slopes with Tignes, up to 3,456 m.
- **Ski level.** 63 % intermediate and 27 % difficult. For confident skiers.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 2 h 30 to 3 h. From Geneva: 2 h 40 to 3 h 30.
- **The kosher side.** Chalets are the usual way to stay, so a chef or delivered meals fit well. The last 32 km are mountain road: plan Friday arrivals with a wide margin before candle lighting.

[Kosher Val d'Isère](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-val-disere/)

### Grenoble

- **Altitude.** 214 m. A city, not a ski resort: your base for the Isère resorts.
- **Ski level.** Depends on the resort you drive to (Alpe d'Huez is 1 h away).
- **The road.** From Lyon: about 1 h by road, or 1 h 04 by direct TGV from the airport. From Geneva: ask us.
- **The kosher side.** A Beth Habad and two synagogues in the city; chef or delivered meals in town or in the resort.

[Kosher Grenoble](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-grenoble/)

### Annecy

- **Altitude.** 447 m, on the lake. In winter, families ski at La Clusaz, 37 minutes away.
- **Ski level.** La Clusaz counts 125 km of slopes, 54 % easy.
- **The road.** From Lyon: 1 h 15. From Geneva: 45 minutes.
- **The kosher side.** A small synagogue, so a minyan for your week is built ahead; chef or delivered meals by the lake or in the Aravis.

[Kosher Annecy](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-annecy/)

## French ski resorts: *questions*

Which French ski resort suits a kosher family with young children?

Les Deux Alpes and La Plagne have the largest share of easy slopes in this guide. In both, our team brings a chef or delivered meals to your chalet or apartment, and Shabbat is ready before candle lighting.

How far are the resorts from Lyon and Geneva?

Most resorts are about two hours from Lyon-Saint-Exupéry without traffic. Megève, Chamonix and Annecy are closer to Geneva, between 45 minutes and 1 h 30. Add time on a winter Saturday or in snow.

Is there a minyan in every resort?

A minyan depends on how many families are in the resort that week. Courchevel 1850 and Megève have a Chabad house in winter. Elsewhere, give us your dates early and we group the families who write to us, with a Sefer Torah.
