Chabad in Lyon
Four Chabad houses, a Shabbat table always open

Lyon has four Chabad centres: in the city centre, in the 6th arrondissement, in Villeurbanne and on the university campus. Wherever you come from, you can call before Shabbat and ask for a meal or a minyan. Our guide is part of this community.

  • 4 Chabad houses
  • Shabbat meals on request
  • Lyon · Villeurbanne · Campus

Beth Habad Centre-Ville

10 rue Mulet, 69001 Lyon

Rabbi Sender Gurewitz

06 21 82 05 56

In the Presqu'île, a few minutes from Vieux Lyon and the Grande Synagogue.

Chabad Loubavitch Lyon 6

60 rue Crillon, 69006 Lyon

Rabbi Mendel Nemanow

In the 6th arrondissement, the neighbourhood of most kosher restaurants on the Lyon side.

Chabad Loubavitch Villeurbanne

The heart of Jewish Villeurbanne: synagogue Beth-Menahem, yeshiva, school and mikveh on the same street.

Chabad on Campus Lyon

10 promenade Léa-et-Napoléon-Bullukian, 69008 Lyon

Rabbi Eliezer Gurewitz

For students and young travellers.

How it works

  • Shabbat meals. Call the centre nearest your hotel before Friday noon and say how many you are. There is no fixed price; a donation is customary.
  • Mikveh. The community mikveh is in Villeurbanne, rue Francis-de-Pressensé. Call Chabad Villeurbanne for hours.
  • With Mishpacha. Tell us when you book and we point you to the right centre for your dates and your neighbourhood.
