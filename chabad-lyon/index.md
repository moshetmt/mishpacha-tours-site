# Chabad in Lyon *Eight Chabad houses, a Shabbat table always open*

Lyon has eight Chabad centres: city centre, 3rd and 6th arrondissements, Villeurbanne, Charpennes, Écully, Craponne and the university campus. Wherever you come from, you can call before Shabbat and ask for a meal or a minyan. Our guide is part of this community.

- 8 Chabad houses
- Shabbat meals on request
- Lyon · Villeurbanne · Écully · Campus

## Beth Habad Centre-Ville

10 rue Mulet, 69001 Lyon

Rabbi Sender Gurewitz

In the Presqu'île, a few minutes from Vieux Lyon and the Grande Synagogue.

## Chabad Loubavitch Lyon 6

60 rue Crillon, 69006 Lyon

Rabbi Mendel Nemanow

In the 6th arrondissement, the neighbourhood of most kosher restaurants on the Lyon side.

## Chabad Loubavitch Villeurbanne

295 rue Francis-de-Pressensé, 69100 Villeurbanne

Rabbi Schneor Zalmen Gurewitz

The heart of Jewish Villeurbanne: synagogue Beth-Menahem, yeshiva and school on the same street.

## Chabad on Campus Lyon

10 promenade Léa-et-Napoléon-Bullukian, 69008 Lyon

Rabbi Eliezer Gurewitz

For students and young travellers.

## Beth Habad Charpennes

86 cours Émile-Zola, 69100 Villeurbanne

Rabbi Haim-Hillel Zekri

At the Charpennes end of Villeurbanne, by the metro, between the 6th arrondissement and Gratte-Ciel.

## Beth Habad Lyon 3

119 rue Servient, 69003 Lyon

Rabbi Eliezer (Lippe) Gurewitz

In the 3rd arrondissement, between Part-Dieu station and the Hypercacher grocery of rue Claudius-Pionchon.

## Beth Habad Écully

67 chemin du Tronchon, 69130 Écully

Rabbi Lévy Gurewitz

West of Lyon, near the Lilly Market grocery of Écully.

## Beth Habad de Craponne

166 avenue Pierre-Auguste Roiret, 69290 Craponne

Rabbi Eliezer (Lippe) Gurewitz

In Craponne, west of Lyon, 25 minutes by car from the city centre.

## How it *works*

- **Shabbat meals.** Tell us your dates and how many you are before Thursday: we put you in touch with the house nearest your hotel. There is no fixed price; a donation is customary.
- **Mikveh.** Lyon and Villeurbanne have several mikvaot; addresses, phones and how to book are on our [mikveh page](https://mishpachatours.com/mikveh-lyon/).
- **With Mishpacha.** Tell us when you book and we point you to the right centre for your dates and your neighbourhood.
