## How it *works*

You come for advice. You leave with a full programme.

1. **Step 1**Tell us your trip

   Your dates, how many adults, children and babies, and a resort if you have one in mind. We answer the same day, never on Shabbat.
2. **Step 2**Receive your programme

   Day by day: arrival, ski, meals, Shabbat, minyan, outings and departure, with photos. The price comes at the end.
3. **Step 3**We organise, you ski

   Chef, transfers, Shabbat and holidays are arranged before you land. One contact on WhatsApp for the whole stay.
