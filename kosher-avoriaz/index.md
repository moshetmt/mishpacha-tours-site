## A resort *without cars*

- **No cars, from the start.** Created in 1966, Avoriaz was designed as a 100% pedestrian village (avoriaz.com). You leave the car in a car park at the entrance, or ride the Prodains cable car up from the valley: 4 minutes, then you go on foot or on skis.
- **A village drawn in one piece.** Architect Jacques Labro designed it in the spirit of organic architecture, with pyramid-shaped buildings.
- **The Portes du Soleil at your door.** Avoriaz sits at the heart of the Portes du Soleil, a ski area linking 12 resorts in France and Switzerland. Morzine, Montriond and Châtel belong to the same area: if you sleep in one of these villages, tell us.
- **Families.** No road to cross with children, and accommodation with the skis at the door. Large families and several families together are our core: send the number of adults and children.

## Getting *to Avoriaz*

- **From Geneva airport:** about 1 h 25 by road, 85 km according to the resort. Shuttles run from the airport to the Prodains cable car or to the reception area of the village. Add time on a winter Saturday.
- **The last stretch, on foot.** Avoriaz has no cars: you park at the entrance or ride the Prodains Express cable car up from the valley (4 minutes), then you walk.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport:** 212 km, about 2 h 40 by road according to the resort. Lyon suits long-haul flights and a first kosher shop before you climb: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.
