A private chef cooks in your chalet, in a kitchen kashered for your stay, and takes care of all the meals of the week. The menu is built with you: tell us what your family eats and what you would like to taste. You ski, you walk, you rest, and a table is set when you come back.

The price starts from €1,568 per day. Beyond three hours by road from Grenoble, in Verbier or elsewhere in Switzerland for example, meals are not delivered, so the private chef is the formula we offer. Switzerland, Italy and Austria are possible on request.

What the chef takes care of

  • A kashered kitchen. The kitchen of the chalet is kashered before the chef arrives, so every dish is prepared in a kosher kitchen.
  • Cooking on site. The chef cooks in your chalet. The meals are prepared there and served at your table.
  • A menu built with you. No standard menu is published. You share your tastes, your family's needs and the dishes you love, and the menu follows.
  • All the meals of the week. The chef takes care of every meal of the week. For Shabbat and festivals, give us your dates and we confirm in writing.
  • Near or far from Grenoble. The formula also works beyond three hours from Grenoble, where meals are not delivered. Tell us where you are staying.
  • One contact, one written quote. One person follows your stay from start to finish. You receive a written quote before any commitment, and we reply the same day, except on Shabbat.

Your questions

How much does a private kosher chef cost?

A private chef starts from €1,568 per day. The price for your dates is sent to you in writing, with the quote, before any commitment.

Is there a standard menu?

No. The menu is built with you. Tell us what your family likes and what you would like to taste, and we build the week together.

Does the chef cook for Shabbat and festivals?

The chef takes care of the meals of the week. Give us your dates and tell us which festival falls during your stay, and we confirm in writing what is prepared for Shabbat and the festival.

Who certifies the kosher?

Kosher lamehadrin, certified by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany. The meals are Chalav Yisrael and the service is shomer Shabbat. The kitchen of your chalet is kashered before the chef cooks.

Can you work in Verbier or elsewhere in Switzerland?

Yes. Beyond three hours from Grenoble, meals are not delivered, so the private chef is the formula we offer. Italy and Austria are possible on request.

Can the chef cook in a chalet I rent elsewhere?

Yes, the chef cooks in your chalet and the kitchen is kashered before the stay. Tell us where it is and send us your dates, and we reply in writing.
