## What the chef *takes care of*

* **A kashered kitchen.** The kitchen of the chalet is kashered before the chef arrives, so every dish is prepared in a kosher kitchen.
* **Cooking on site.** The chef cooks in your chalet. The meals are prepared there and served at your table.
* **A menu built with you.** No standard menu is published. You share your tastes, your family's needs and the dishes you love, and the menu follows.
* **All the meals of the week.** The chef takes care of every meal of the week. For Shabbat and festivals, give us your dates and we confirm in writing.
* **Near or far from Grenoble.** The formula also works beyond three hours from Grenoble, where meals are not delivered. Tell us where you are staying.
* **One contact, one written quote.** One person follows your stay from start to finish. You receive a written quote before any commitment, and we reply the same day, except on Shabbat.
