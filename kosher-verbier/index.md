# Verbier, strictly kosher *on the sunny balcony of the 4 Vallées*

## What we organise *in Verbier*

- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and the chef cooks on site, every meal of the week. In winter you come back from the 4 Vallées to a set table; in summer, from the bike trails or a concert of the festival.
- **The kosher shopping, brought in.** Meat, wine, challot and dry goods travel with the chef, declared at the Swiss border where needed; the rest is bought in the kosher shops of Geneva or Lausanne on the way up. Tell us where you sleep: in the village, higher up on the slopes, or down in Le Châble, because the road up adds minutes in snow.
- **Shabbat.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine are cooked and set before candle lighting. Nothing is prepared on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **The holidays.** Hanukkah, Purim, Pesach or Sukkot in the Valais. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** No synagogue is listed in Verbier, so a minyan is built from the families who write to us for the same week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.
- **The chalet alone.** If you prefer to cook yourselves, we find the chalet and tell you where to buy kosher food before you climb. Ask for it as such.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Chalet, chef, transfers from Geneva, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core, including large groups and Hebrew-speaking guests. Verbier is more than three hours from our kitchen in the French Alps, so we do not deliver meals there: the chef comes to you.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. Detail of each service: [kosher services in the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-services-french-alps/).

## Kosher *in Verbier*

Supervision is rarely written down, so check before you book.

- **Kosher restaurant, shop or hotel.** None found in the resort. Plan on your chef, or bring your food.
- **Synagogue and Chabad house.** None is listed in Verbier or in the Val de Bagnes. The nearest synagogue in the Valais is in [Crans-Montana](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-crans-montana/), open by season.
- **Jewish guests.** The Swiss Federation of Jewish Communities added Verbier to its summer project for Jewish visitors in 2022, so Jewish families do come, in summer as in winter.
- **Nearest large communities.** Geneva and Lausanne, both on the road from Geneva airport, have synagogues, kosher shops and caterers. Plan any shopping there before you drive up.
- **Season.** The Verbier lifts are announced from 5 December 2026 to 25 April 2027, with weekend openings in November if snow allows. The whole 4 Vallées area is announced from 12 December 2026 to 18 April 2027.

## Winter and summer *in Verbier*

- **Winter.** Verbier opens onto the 4 Vallées, about 410 km of runs linked with Nendaz, Veysonnaz, Thyon and La Tzoumaz, the largest ski area in Switzerland. Mont Fort tops it at 3,330 m. Sunset comes early on a Friday in December, so Shabbat meals are set well before the lifts close.
- **Summer.** More than 500 km of walking trails and more than 800 km of mountain bike trails start from the village. The Verbier Festival brings classical concerts from 15 July to 1 August 2027. Packed lunches for the trail are prepared by the chef with notice.
- **Minyan and Shabbat.** With no synagogue in the resort, every minyan in Verbier is built from the families who write to us, with a Sefer Torah. Shabbat times appear live on the Alps pages of this site.

## Getting *to Verbier*

- **From Geneva airport:** about 1 h 50 by road along the lake and the Rhône valley, then up from Le Châble.
- **By train:** about 2 h 40 from Geneva, changing at Martigny for Le Châble. On winter weekends and public holidays, direct trains run to Le Châble.
- **From Le Châble:** the cable car climbs to Verbier in about 20 minutes and runs from 5:10 am to 11:55 pm; a bus does the same trip at the same fare.
- **From Sion:** about 50 minutes by car.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry:** about 3 h 40 by road through Geneva. Lyon suits long-haul flights and a first kosher shop before you climb: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
- **Crossing from France:** carry a passport or identity card for every traveller, children included, and buy the Swiss motorway vignette online before the border, as the road from Geneva uses Swiss motorways. The kosher products travel with the chef and are declared at the border where needed.
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

## Kosher in Verbier: *questions*

How much does it cost?

It depends on the dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit.

Do you deliver kosher meals to Verbier?

No. Verbier is more than three hours from our kitchen in the French Alps, so meals are not delivered there. A private chef comes to your chalet with the kosher shopping and cooks on site, or you take the chalet alone and cook yourselves.

Is there a minyan in Verbier?

No synagogue is listed in the resort. A minyan depends on how many families are in Verbier that week. Give us your dates early and we group them, with a Sefer Torah.

Do you work in summer as well as in winter?

Yes. Verbier is open to walkers, bikers and festival guests in summer and to skiers in winter, and the services are the same: private chef, Shabbat, holidays, minyan.

Can you organise a stay for a large group?

Yes. Groups and families from abroad are our core. Send the number of people, the dates and what you need, and you receive one written quote.

Who supervises the food?

Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come with your quote.
