# Things to do in Lyon *for a Jewish family that keeps Shabbat*

Give Lyon two or three days: Vieux Lyon and its traboules first, the Parc de la Tête d’Or with children next, a museum for the rainy afternoon. Fourteen places below, each with the time it takes, whether it suits children, where kosher food is nearby and how it fits Shabbat. The Grande Synagogue is the one visit where we take you inside a building that is closed to the public.

## Old Lyon *and its hills*

- **Vieux Lyon: a walk through the traboules.** A UNESCO-listed Renaissance quarter between the Saône and the hill.  
  2–3 h · Children yes · Kosher 2nd arr., 10 minRead more

  A Renaissance quarter between the Saône and the hill, listed by UNESCO in 1998. Walk rue Saint-Jean and rue du Bœuf, then [rue Juiverie](https://mishpachatours.com/rue-juiverie-lyon/), the street that kept the name of the medieval Jewish community. Traboules are covered passages that cut through the buildings from one street to the next. Some stay open in the daytime; a closed door belongs to the residents, so leave it.  
  **Time:** 2 to 3 hours. **Children:** yes, the passages are a game for them; the cobbles are hard on strollers. **Kosher:** restaurants of the Presqu’île and the 2nd arrondissement, 10 minutes on foot. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** the streets are always open; we guide Sunday to Friday.
- **Fourvière: two thousand years of Roman history.** The Roman theatres of Lugdunum, capital of the Gauls, stand on the hill above Vieux Lyon.  
  1–2 h · Children yes · Kosher none on the hillRead more

  The Roman theatres sit on the hill above Vieux Lyon, on the site of Lugdunum, the capital of the Gauls. The Grand Théâtre dates from about 15 BC and the open-air site is free to enter. Take the F2 funicular from Vieux Lyon to the Minimes-Théâtres romains station. The Lugdunum museum next door is open Tuesday to Sunday.  
  **Time:** 1 to 2 hours. **Children:** yes, they climb the tiers; the hill is steep, so take the funicular. **Kosher:** none on the hill; eat in the 2nd arrondissement before you climb. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** free access every day, 7:00 to 21:00 from 15 April to 15 September and to 19:00 the rest of the year; we guide Sunday to Friday.
- **Croix-Rousse: a walk up to the silk weavers’ wall.** The silk weavers’ hill and its 1,200 m² painted wall, the Mur des Canuts.  
  1–2 h · Children yes · Kosher Presqu’île, belowRead more

  The hill of the canuts, the silk weavers. The Mur des Canuts, on boulevard des Canuts, is a trompe-l’œil painted over 1,200 m² that shows the neighbourhood as it lived. Take metro line C to Croix-Rousse instead of climbing the slopes. Rue Sainte-Catherine, where our [Jewish Lyon tour](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/jewish-lyon/) ends, runs down the same hill.  
  **Time:** 1 to 2 hours. **Children:** yes, with the metro for the climb. **Kosher:** down the hill, on the Presqu’île. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** the wall is outdoors and always visible; we guide Sunday to Friday.

## Parks, squares *and rivers*

- **Parc de la Tête d’Or: a lake, a free zoo, rose gardens.** Lyon’s best place for children: 105 free hectares, a free zoo, rose gardens and boats on the lake.  
  3 h · Children yes · Kosher 6th arr. · zoo closed TuesdayRead more

  The best place in Lyon for children. The park covers 105 hectares and is free. The zoo is free too, as are the three rose gardens and the botanical garden. Boats and pedal boats go out on the lake when the weather allows.  
  **Time:** 3 hours or half a day. **Children:** yes, the first choice. **Kosher:** the 6th arrondissement borders the park. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** the park is open every day, 6:30 to 20:30 (to 22:30 from 15 April to 14 October). The zoo opens 9:30 to 17:00 (17:45 in summer) and closes on Tuesday, so plan another day.
- **Presqu’île and Place Bellecour: a walk between two rivers.** The peninsula between the Rhône and the Saône, from the vast Place Bellecour to place des Terreaux along rue de la République.  
  2 h · Children yes · Kosher 2nd arr.Read more

  The peninsula between the Rhône and the Saône. Place Bellecour, one of the largest open squares in Europe, has Louis XIV on horseback at its centre. Walk north along rue de la République to place des Terreaux and the town hall.  
  **Time:** 2 hours. **Children:** yes, there is room to run. **Kosher:** the restaurants of the 2nd arrondissement are a few minutes away. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** the square is always open; most shops close on Sunday, so Sunday suits squares and quays better than shopping.
- **Fresque des Lyonnais: thirty figures of local history.** An 800 m² painted wall with about thirty figures from Lyon’s history, from the Lumière brothers to Saint-Exupéry.  
  20 minutes · Children yes · Kosher Presqu’îleRead more

  A painted wall of about 800 m² on the quai de la Pêcherie, at the corner of rue de la Martinière, with around thirty people from two thousand years of Lyon’s history, from the Lumière brothers to Antoine de Saint-Exupéry. Children play spot the person.  
  **Time:** 20 minutes. **Children:** yes. **Kosher:** the Presqu’île, near Vieux Lyon. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** outdoors and always open; we guide Sunday to Friday.
- **The quays: an hour by bike along two rivers.** Riverside paths away from traffic along both rivers, with Vélo’v self-service bikes.  
  1–2 h · Children yes · Kosher Presqu’îleRead more

  Both rivers have riverside paths away from the traffic. The Rhône banks are wide, with lawns and flat paths; the Saône quays are quieter and face Vieux Lyon. Vélo’v self-service bikes stand in stations along both. Walk instead if you prefer.  
  **Time:** 1 to 2 hours. **Children:** yes, the Rhône banks are flat and away from cars. **Kosher:** both banks reach the Presqu’île. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** the paths are always open; we do not ride on Shabbat, a walk on the banks suits Friday afternoon.
- **Part-Dieu: the shopping centre.** Lyon’s main shopping centre, for everything you forgot to pack.  
  1–2 h · Children yes · Kosher 3rd arr. · shops Monday to SaturdayRead more

  Lyon’s main shopping centre, around the station in the 3rd arrondissement: clothes, shoes, chargers and everything you forgot. Shops open Monday to Saturday from 9:30 to 20:00, and on some Sundays. A certified kosher grocery for Shabbat shopping lies in the same arrondissement.  
  **Time:** 1 to 2 hours. **Children:** yes, it is a mall. **Kosher:** order your Shabbat food before Thursday. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** shops open on Saturday; we shop Sunday to Friday.

## Museums *for a rainy day*

- **Confluences museum: science and societies.** A glass and steel museum of natural sciences and human societies, where the Rhône and the Saône meet.  
  2–3 h · Children yes · Kosher 2nd arr. · closed MondayRead more

  A glass and steel building at the southern tip of the Presqu’île, where the Rhône and the Saône meet, at 86 quai Perrache. Natural sciences and human societies, with temporary exhibitions.  
  **Time:** 2 to 3 hours. **Children:** yes; choose a few galleries rather than the whole museum. **Kosher:** eat in the 2nd arrondissement before or after; the museum is about 2.5 km south of Bellecour. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:30 to 18:30 (also Monday in zone A school holidays outside summer). We visit on Tuesday to Friday or Sunday.
- **Institut Lumière: the family villa and the first film.** The birthplace of cinema: the Lumière family villa and the factory site where one of the first films was shot in 1895.  
  1.5–2 h · Children from about 8 · Kosher 3rd or 6th arr. · closed MondayRead more

  The birthplace of cinema, at 25 rue du Premier-Film in the 8th arrondissement: the Lumière family villa and the site of the factory where the brothers shot one of the first films in 1895.  
  **Time:** 1.5 to 2 hours. **Children:** from about eight. **Kosher:** eat before you go, in the 3rd or the 6th arrondissement; metro line D reaches Monplaisir-Lumière. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:30. We visit on Tuesday to Friday or Sunday.
- **Musée des Beaux-Arts: a visit through 70 rooms.** Art from antiquity to today in 70 rooms, with a sculpture garden, on place des Terreaux.  
  2 h · Children from about 10 · Kosher Presqu’île · closed TuesdayRead more

  Art from antiquity to today in 70 rooms and 7,000 m², at 20 place des Terreaux, with a sculpture garden.  
  **Time:** 2 hours. **Children:** from about ten, with a short route chosen in advance. **Kosher:** the Presqu’île, a few minutes away. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** open Wednesday to Monday, 10:00 to 18:00 (10:30 on Friday), closed on Tuesday. We visit on any other day, Sunday to Friday.

## Jewish Lyon *and memory*

- **Grande Synagogue: the building we take you inside.** Inaugurated in 1864 and closed to the public, it is the one building we take you inside, on our 2 h 30 Jewish Lyon tour, subject to opening hours and security.  
  2 h 30 with the tour · Children from about 8 · Kosher 2nd arr. · no visitors on ShabbatRead more

  Inaugurated in 1864 at 13 quai Tilsitt, it is still a working synagogue and is closed to visitors outside the Heritage Days. Mishpacha Tours holds the special authorisation and takes private groups inside on its 2 h 30 [Jewish Lyon tour](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/jewish-lyon/), subject to opening hours and security on the day. It is the one visit on this page that you cannot make alone. [The Grande Synagogue, in detail](https://mishpachatours.com/grande-synagogue-lyon/).  
  **Time:** 2 h 30 with the tour. **Children:** from about eight. **Kosher:** the 2nd arrondissement, a short walk. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** services are held, but visitors are not admitted; our visits run Sunday to Friday morning.
- **CHRD: the memory museum.** The resistance and deportation museum, in the former Gestapo headquarters.  
  2 h 30 · Children teenagers · Kosher 6th or 2nd arr. · closed Monday and TuesdayRead more

  The Centre d’histoire de la résistance et de la déportation, at 14 avenue Berthelot, in the former Gestapo headquarters of Lyon. Our private visit follows the Jewish thread of the occupation: the raids, the deportations, the children of Izieu. [The CHRD, in detail](https://mishpachatours.com/chrd-lyon/).  
  **Time:** 2 h 30. **Children:** teenagers; for younger ones, talk to us first. **Kosher:** the 6th or the 2nd arrondissement, by metro. See [Kosher in Lyon](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-restaurants-lyon/). **Shabbat:** open Wednesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00, closed Monday and Tuesday. We visit on Wednesday to Friday or Sunday.
- **Izieu: a full-day visit.** The memorial to the 44 Jewish children arrested on 6 April 1944, one hour from Lyon by road.  
  a full day · Children from about 8 · Kosher bring your own food · check the memorial’s hoursRead more

  The Maison d’Izieu is the memorial to the 44 Jewish children arrested on 6 April 1944, one hour from Lyon by road. We spend the day there with a van and driver. The main access bridge is closed for works until November 2026, with a signed detour. [The Maison d’Izieu, in detail](https://mishpachatours.com/maison-izieu/).  
  **Time:** a full day. **Children:** from about eight, the memorial’s minimum age. **Kosher:** bring your own kosher food; picnics are allowed in the orchard. **Shabbat:** the memorial opens on Saturday from April to August; we visit Sunday to Friday.

## Three days, *three ways*

**A family with children: three days.**  
Day 1: Vieux Lyon, then the Fresque and Place Bellecour.  
Day 2: Tête d’Or, with a boat on the lake (not on Tuesday).  
Day 3: Lumière or Confluences (not on Monday), then the quays.  
  
**A couple: a slower walk, three days.**  
Day 1: Vieux Lyon and rue Juiverie, then the Roman site.  
Day 2: Croix-Rousse and the Mur des Canuts, then the Beaux-Arts.  
Day 3: Grande Synagogue and CHRD with our guide, Wednesday to Friday.  
  
**Passing through on the way to the Alps.**  
One day: our [Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/), three hours with the Grande Synagogue and a kosher lunch.  
Two days: add Vieux Lyon and Tête d’Or.  
Then a [van](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-van-transfers/) to [the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/alps/).

## Visiting Lyon: *questions*

How many days do I need in Lyon?

Two days cover Vieux Lyon, the Parc de la Tête d’Or and one museum. Three days add the Grande Synagogue and a Memory visit. Izieu takes a full day on top.

What is free in Lyon?

The Parc de la Tête d’Or with its zoo and rose gardens, the Roman site on Fourvière, the Mur des Canuts, the Fresque des Lyonnais, the quays and the traboules that stay open.

What is open on Shabbat?

Parks, squares and quays are open every day, and the museums on this page open on Saturday. We guide Sunday to Friday.

What do we do if it rains?

Go to the CHRD, the Confluences, the Beaux-Arts or the Institut Lumière, or walk the covered traboules of Vieux Lyon. The [Lyon weather](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-weather/) page has the seven-day forecast and a plan for each kind of day.
