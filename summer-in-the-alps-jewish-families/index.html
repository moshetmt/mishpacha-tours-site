Summer in the Alps for Jewish families
lakes, hikes and a minyan within reach

Summer in the Alps means lakes, walking and long evenings.

Plan my tripOr message us on WhatsApp

For an observant family it also means working out where the minyan is, who feeds you on Shabbat and what is open. Three towns near Lyon have a Jewish community: Aix-les-Bains, Annecy and Grenoble. In the resort, our kosher team organises meals, Shabbat and a minyan for your dates: one request, one quote. We sell no packages and no stays.

Three towns with a Jewish community

  • Aix-les-Bains On the lake of Le Bourget, 1 h 15 from Lyon by train. It also shows a family offering home-made meals for visitors.
  • Annecy On its lake, 1 h 45 from Lyon by car. One community centre and synagogue, with a rabbinical delegate.
  • Grenoble 1 h 15 from Lyon by train, at the foot of the Chartreuse and Vercors. Two synagogues and the Beth Habad that heads the Chabad network of the Alps.

High resorts in summer

  • Courchevel and Megève. Nothing says they run in summer. Write to Rabbi Daniel Belaïch (habadalpes@yahoo.fr) before you count on one.
  • Chamonix. Its trips are designed for adults over 50, so they do not suit a family with children. Dates are on its website.
  • Evian and Lake Geneva. Geneva has its own community, across the border.

Hiking and Shabbat in the mountains

Summer plans fail on small things. These points help.

  • Choose where you sleep before the trail. Stay within walking distance of the minyan, or of the host who feeds you.
  • Cook ahead. Prepare the Friday meal and bring it, or order it before Thursday from the community. Few mountain villages have a kosher shop.
  • Check candle lighting. Summer candle lighting in Savoie is later than in winter, and a late hike ends on Friday afternoon at the latest.
  • Write down the nearest minyan. Before you lose the network in the valley.
  • Carry water and a map. Do not start a long route on Friday.

For rulings, ask your own rabbi. We give practical points only.

Summer in the Alps: questions

Which Alpine town has the best-equipped Jewish community near Lyon?

Aix-les-Bains is the most documented: synagogues, a mikveh, shops, restaurants and caterers are all announced on its community website, and a family offers home-made meals for visitors.

Is there a Chabad house in Annecy?

The Beth Habad of Annemasse, 40 minutes away, is the nearest. The Annecy community centre has a rabbinical delegate.

Do the Chabad services of Courchevel and Megève run in summer?

Write to Rabbi Belaïch before you plan around one.

Can we keep Shabbat while hiking?

Plan lodging near a minyan, cook before Shabbat and finish your walk well before candle lighting. For a ruling, ask your rabbi.

Can we combine Lyon with the Alps?

Yes. Land in Lyon, take a 2 h 30 city tour or the 3 hour Stopover, and add Izieu. Grenoble and Aix-les-Bains are 1 h 15 from Lyon by train, Chambéry 1 h 20.
