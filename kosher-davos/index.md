# Kosher Davos *chef and Shabbat at 1,560 m*

Davos sits at 1,560 m in the Grisons, the highest town in the Alps, below the Parsenn and the Jakobshorn.

## What we organise *in Davos*

- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and the chef cooks on site, every meal of the week. In winter you come back from the Parsenn or the Jakobshorn to a set table; in summer, from the lake or the trails.
- **The kosher shopping, brought in.** Meat, wine, challot and dry goods travel with the chef, declared at the Swiss border where needed; the rest is bought in the kosher shops of Zürich, about two hours away. Tell us where you sleep: Davos Platz, Davos Dorf, or one of the side valleys.
- **Shabbat.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine are cooked and set before candle lighting. Nothing is prepared on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **The holidays.** Hanukkah, Purim, Pesach or Sukkot in the Grisons. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** Jewish families have come to Davos for more than a century, and in high season you are rarely the only observant family in town. We build the minyan from the families who write to us for the same week: give us your dates early, with a Sefer Torah on request.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Chalet, chef, transfers from Zürich, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core, including large groups and Hebrew-speaking guests. Davos is far more than three hours from our kitchen in the French Alps, so we do not deliver meals there: the chef comes to you.

Strictly kosher. Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. Detail of each service: [kosher services in the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-services-french-alps/).

## Kosher *in Davos*

Supervision is rarely written down, so check before you book.

- **Kosher restaurant, shop or hotel.** Seasonal at best, and not every year. We do not depend on it: plan on your chef, or bring your food.
- **Synagogue and Chabad house.** No permanent synagogue is listed in Davos. The nearest community is Zürich, about two hours away, with synagogues, kosher shops and caterers.
- **Jewish guests.** Davos has welcomed Jewish visitors since its days as a spa town; in winter, observant families are a familiar sight on the Promenade.
- **January.** The World Economic Forum fills the town for one week in January: book very early for that week, or choose another.
- **Season.** The Davos Klosters lifts usually run from late November to mid-April, the Jakobshorn a little later. Check the dates for your week before you book.

## Winter and summer *in Davos*

- **Winter.** Six ski areas around Davos and Klosters, about 300 km of runs: the Parsenn with the Weissfluhgipfel at 2,844 m, the Jakobshorn, Pischa, Rinerhorn, Madrisa and the Schatzalp. Sunset comes early on a Friday in December, so Shabbat meals are set well before the lifts close.
- **Summer.** Some 700 km of walking trails, the Davos lake, mountain biking, and the Davos Festival of young musicians in August. Packed lunches for the trail are prepared by the chef with notice.
- **Minyan and Shabbat.** Every minyan in Davos is built from the families who write to us, with a Sefer Torah. Shabbat times appear live on this page.

## Getting *to Davos*

- **From Zürich airport:** about 2 h by road through Landquart and the Prättigau.
- **By train:** about 2 h 15 from Zürich airport, changing at Landquart for the Rhaetian Railway to Davos Dorf and Davos Platz.
- **From Geneva airport:** about 4 h 30 by road; a long day, better with a stop in Zürich for the kosher shopping.
- **From Milan:** about 3 h 30 by road.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry:** about 5 h 30 by road. For Davos, Zürich is the natural airport; Lyon suits a stay that starts in the French Alps: [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
- **Crossing into Switzerland:** carry a passport or identity card for every traveller, children included, and buy the Swiss motorway vignette online before the border. The kosher products travel with the chef and are declared at the border where needed.

## Kosher in Davos: *questions*

How much does it cost?

It depends on the dates, the number of people and the menu. You receive a written quote before you commit.

Do you deliver kosher meals to Davos?

No. Davos is far more than three hours from our kitchen in the French Alps, so meals are not delivered there. A private chef comes to your chalet with the kosher shopping and cooks on site.

Is there a minyan in Davos?

No permanent synagogue is listed. In high season a minyan is easier to build in Davos than in most resorts, because observant families come every winter. Give us your dates early and we group the families who write to us, with a Sefer Torah on request.

Do you work in summer as well as in winter?

Yes. Davos is open to walkers, bikers and festival guests in summer and to skiers in winter, and the services are the same: private chef, Shabbat, holidays, minyan.

Can you organise a stay for a large group?

Yes. Groups and families from abroad are our core. Send the number of people, the dates and what you need, and you receive one written quote.

Who supervises the food?

Our kosher team based in the Alps cooks under the Chabad network of the Alps. The supervision details come with your quote.
