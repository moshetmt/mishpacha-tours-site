- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. Val d'Isère is a chalet resort: the chef works in the kitchen you rent, and every meal of the week is taken care of.

## Getting to *Val d'Isère*

- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry.** 220 km, 2 h 30 to 3 h by road without traffic. Add an hour on a Saturday in February or in snow.
- **From Geneva.** 225 km. Transfer companies quote between 2 h 40 and 3 h 30, via Annecy and Albertville, so Lyon is the easier arrival for the Alps.
- **By train.** Bourg-Saint-Maurice is the station for the valley. The resort is then 32 km up the mountain road, 45 minutes by bus.
- **Shuttles.** Winter shuttles run from Lyon airport; details on [Lyon, the gateway to the Alps](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/). Transfers can be part of a tailor-made stay, on quote.
- **Kosher groceries before you climb.** Lyon has certified shops: [what to buy and where](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/).
- **Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?** [The Stopover](https://mishpachatours.com/tours/stopover/): a private 3 hour tour of Jewish Lyon between the plane and the road.

## Skiing, *in short*

- **One ski area with Tignes.** The Tignes-Val d'Isère domain, formerly Espace Killy, offers 300 km of pistes. The highest point, 3,456 m, is on the Tignes side, at the Grande Motte.
- **Summer skiing.** The Pissaillas glacier usually opens in early June, and in recent years the lifts have closed by mid-July.
