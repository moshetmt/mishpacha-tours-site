# Privacy *what we keep, and why*

We collect what we need to answer you and to run your tour, nothing else. This page says what that is, where it is stored and how to have it deleted.

- Data kept 3 years

## Who is *responsible*

Mishpacha Tours, a sole-trader business run by the guide in Lyon, France. Contact: [contact@mishpachatours.com](mailto:contact@mishpachatours.com), or the request form. Business details are in the [legal notice](https://mishpachatours.com/legal/).

## What we *collect*

- **When you send the request form:** the trip or tour you want, your dates, the number of people, the language, your name, your phone or WhatsApp number, and your email address if you give one. The form also asks for details that help us prepare your quote: dietary needs and allergies, the ages of your children, your level of religious observance (minyan and kashrut) and your budget. The form writes a line in a private Google Sheet and sends us an email.
- **Legal basis:** steps prior to a contract for the booking details. For allergies and dietary needs (health data) and for religious observance, we rely on your explicit consent (article 9.2.a of the GDPR), given by ticking the box under the Send button of the form. Without that consent the form cannot be sent.
- **When you write on WhatsApp or by email:** your messages and your number or address, kept in those services under their own terms.
- **When you visit the site:** before you press Accept, nothing from us: no cookie and no third-party script. After Accept, Google Analytics and Google Ads load, as described in the section Cookies and measurement below. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard server logs for security. Map pages load tiles from OpenStreetMap and Shabbat times from Hebcal; those services see your IP address when the page loads.

## Who *receives* it

For a stay in the Alps, we pass your request to the service provider in the Alps who prepares the quote (chalet, chef, minyan, transfers). This provider receives your answers, including diet and religious observance, and uses them only to build your quote. Besides the provider, only the services that store the data for us see it (Google, WhatsApp, Vercel). We never sell or share your details, and we never send newsletters.

## How long *we keep it*

Requests and correspondence, including the answers on diet and religious observance, are kept for three years after our last exchange, then deleted.

## Your *rights*

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [contact@mishpachatours.com](mailto:contact@mishpachatours.com); we then delete the answers on health and religious observance, and what was done before is not affected. We answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority, at cnil.fr.

## Cookies and *measurement*

Cookies only with your consent. Before you press Accept, the site loads no third-party script and sets no cookie. After Accept, two Google services load: Google Analytics 4 (audience statistics: page views, steps of the request form, clicks on the WhatsApp and email links) and Google Ads (conversion measurement: which ad led to a request). They set the cookies \_ga and \_ga\_\* (Analytics, 13 months) and \_gcl\_au (Google Ads, 90 days). We do not use them for remarketing or to personalise ads. For enhanced conversions, when you send a request, your email address and your phone number are passed to Google in hashed form (SHA-256, one-way) so that Google can match your request with the ad you clicked; we never pass your name. Google may process this data outside the European Union, under the safeguards described in its own policies. Your choice is kept for six months in your browser (not as a cookie). To withdraw it, click "Cookies" at the bottom of any page and choose Refuse: the Google cookies are deleted and nothing is measured any more.
