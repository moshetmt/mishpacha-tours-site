## What we organise *in Les Arcs*

- **A chalet or apartment with a private chef.** Our kosher team based in the Alps makes the kitchen kosher and cooks on site. You come back from the slopes, or from the trail in summer, to a set table.
- **Meals delivered in the resort.** Hot meals brought to your chalet, apartment or hotel for the week, for a family or a group, with notice. Tell us which village you sleep in: Arc 1600, 1800, 1950, 2000 or Peisey-Vallandry.
- **Shabbat.** Shabbat meals, challot and wine delivered on Friday and set before candle lighting. Nothing is delivered on Shabbat or on a holiday.
- **The holidays.** Hanukkah, Purim or Pesach on the mountain, Sukkot in summer. Tell us which holiday falls in your week.
- **A minyan and a Sefer Torah.** The size of a minyan depends on how many families are in Les Arcs that week. Give us your dates and we group the families who write to us.
- **A tailor-made stay, A to Z.** Chalet, chef or delivered meals, transfers, Shabbat, the holidays, a minyan: one request, one quote. Families and groups from abroad are our core, including large groups and Hebrew-speaking guests. For a very small group asking for one delivery only, say so and we tell you what is feasible.

## Four villages, *one valley*

- **Arc 1600.** Opened in 1968, quieter and suited to families. The funicular from Bourg-Saint-Maurice station arrives here in 7 minutes.
- **Arc 1800.** The lively one, with four pedestrian villages and views of Mont Blanc and the Tarentaise valley.
- **Arc 1950.** A village with North American-inspired architecture, where you can keep your skis on in winter.
- **Arc 2000.** The highest village, with ski-in ski-out access and views of the 3,226 m Aiguille Rouge.
- **Paradiski.** The Vanoise Express cable car crosses the valley to La Plagne and joins the two ski areas, 425 km of pistes together.
- **Minyan and Shabbat.** A minyan is built from the families who write to us for your dates, with a Sefer Torah.

## Getting *to Les Arcs*

- **By train.** Bourg-Saint-Maurice, at 850 m, has the station for Les Arcs. The funicular next to it takes 7 minutes to Arc 1600, and free shuttles serve the other villages. Direct TGV trains from Paris take 4 h 30 to 5 h.
- **From London.** Eurostar Snow runs on Saturdays from London St Pancras to Bourg-Saint-Maurice in just under 8 hours, with one change. Dates for winter 2026-2027 were not on sale on 8 October 2026.
- **From Lyon-Saint-Exupéry airport:** 203 to 212 km, 2 h 25 to 3 h, to Bourg-Saint-Maurice. The climb to the higher villages adds time.
- **From Geneva airport:** 2 h 30 to 3 h by road to Bourg-Saint-Maurice.
- **Season.** Confirm on lesarcs.com before you book a lift pass.
- **Kosher groceries before you climb.** Lyon has certified shops: [what to buy and where](https://mishpachatours.com/lyon-gateway-to-the-alps-kosher/). Land in Lyon with a few hours to spare?
