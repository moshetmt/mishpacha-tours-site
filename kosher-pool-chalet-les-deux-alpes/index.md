## The chalet in pictures

## The chalet, in short

- **Location.** In the heart of Les Deux Alpes, a few steps from the slopes.
- **The living room.** A fireplace, a library corner and a dining table for up to 15 guests, with a semi-open kitchen.
- **Seven bedrooms,** six double rooms and a dormitory with bunk beds. Six bathrooms.
- **Pool and sauna.** A heated indoor pool, a sauna and a shower, on the lower level.
- **Cinema and gym.** A cinema room with a 4K projector and 5.1 sound, and a gym.
- **Ski room.** A ski room with boot and glove dryers.
- **Kitchen.** Kashered by our team before you arrive, included. A chef cooks in the chalet, or kosher meals arrive ready to reheat.

The chalet welcomes up to 15 guests. Availability is confirmed for your dates with each request.

## Shabbat in the chalet

The chef cooks your weekday, Shabbat and holiday meals in the chalet, or your meals are delivered; for Shabbat, they arrive before candle lighting. For a minyan, we bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah: with the men of your group, Shacharis takes place in your living room.

Strictly kosher, everything served is Chalav Yisrael. The kitchen is certified kosher by Beth Habad Grenoble-Alpes, Rav Yehia Lahiany.

For Pesach, we prepare the chalet too: [Pesach in the Alps, private chalet and kashered kitchen](https://mishpachatours.com/kosher-pesach-french-alps/).

## Chalet Neige Éternelle: questions

- **How much does it cost?** Price on request for the chalet. The final price depends on your dates, the number of people and the services you choose: you receive a written quote before you commit to anything. The stay is quoted and invoiced by our kosher team based in the Alps.
- **How many people can stay?** Up to 15 guests: seven bedrooms, six bathrooms and a dining table for 15. One large family, or several families together.
- **Are the slopes far?** The chalet is in the heart of the resort, a few steps from the slopes.
- **Is there a pool?** Yes. A heated indoor pool, with a sauna in the same space, on the lower level.
- **Who cooks the meals?** A chef cooks kosher meals in the chalet, or ready meals are delivered. Our team kashers the kitchen before you arrive.
- **Is there a minyan?** We bring the bochurim and a Sefer Torah to the chalet for Shabbat, on request. Tell us your dates early.
