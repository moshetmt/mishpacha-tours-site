## Pesach in the Alps: *questions*

When is Pesach 2027?

The first seder is on the evening of Wednesday 21 April 2027 (15 Nisan 5787), the second on the evening of Thursday 22 April. Pesach runs from Thursday 22 to Thursday 29 April 2027: eight days, as kept in the diaspora. Dates from the Hebcal calendar.
